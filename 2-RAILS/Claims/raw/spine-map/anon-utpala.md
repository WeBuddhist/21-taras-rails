# Spine map — anon-utpala

> Routing index only. This file records **which of this commentary's own TOC nodes hold
> which canonical spine slot's content** — nothing about what the claims say. It never
> quotes, interprets, or restates a claim; `assemble_packet.py` copies the claim text
> itself verbatim out of `2-RAILS/Claims/raw/tree-guided/anon-utpala.md` at packet time.
> Every claim in that file has exactly one disposition below.

**Structure of this commentary.** A threefold tree under node `1`: brief teaching (`1.1`,
exegesis of the pre-stanza invocation line, not one of the twenty-one homages), extended
explanation (`1.2`), and the teaching of benefits (`1.3` → `benefits`). `1.2` itself divides
into origin (`1.2.1` → `tara-01`), body-forms (`1.2.2`), and activity (`1.2.3`). Body-forms
divides into enjoyment-body — peaceful (`1.2.2.1.1.1`–`.6` → `tara-02`–`tara-07`) and
wrathful (`1.2.2.1.2.1`–`.7` → `tara-08`–`tara-14`) — and truth-body (`1.2.2.2` → `tara-15`,
one node, one verse). Activity divides into six nodes (`1.2.3.1`–`.6` → `tara-16`–`tara-21`).
Every leaf node's correspondence is attested by its own "Root verse quoted" claim, matched
against `1-SOURCES/Text/སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་གི་བསྟོད་པ།.md`'s `^1-1`–`^1-21` (e.g. `1.2.2.1.1.4`
quotes "TUTTĀRA HŪṂ fills the realms, treads the seven worlds," matching `^1-5` verbatim).
Several ancestor nodes in this tree carry **no claims of their own** — the extraction
attributes each ancestor's own division-announcement sentence to the opening claim(s) of its
first-born descendant instead (documented in the raw claims file's own Coverage Log, e.g.
"Also carries the divisions for its three ancestor nodes (1.2.2, 1.2.2.1, 1.2.2.1.1)" at
node `1.2.2.1.1.1`). Mapping such a descendant node whole would sweep its ancestor's
division-statement into a single Tārā's packet, so those four nodes (`1.2.1`,
`1.2.2.1.1.1`, `1.2.2.1.2.1`, `1.2.3.1`) are **not** given Slot map rows; every one of their
claims is routed individually below — the division-statement claim(s) to `structure`, the
rest to the matching `tara-NN`.
