**Structure of this commentary.** A three-part top-level tree. Node `1` (མཚན་དོན་གྱི་སྟེང་ནས་
སྤྱིར་བསྟན་པ) is the commentary's own preliminary etymological analysis of the name "Tārā,"
entirely outside the spine. Node `2` (གཉིས་པ་རྒྱུད་ཀྱི་ཚིག་དོན་བྱེ་བྲག་ཏུ་བཤད་པ) is where the
twenty-one homages sit, each as its own leaf child `2.1`–`2.21`, titled by ordinal plus the
homage's distinguishing "praise-gate" (e.g. `2.1` "first, by way of narrative history"; `2.21`
"twenty-first, by way of complete [buddha-]activity"). Correspondence to the root text's
`^1-1`–`^1-21` is attested by each node's own opening claim, which states its ordinal among
"the twenty-one verses" in so many words (e.g. `c-2-1-1` "of the twenty-one verses… the
first is…"; `c-2-21-1` "…the twenty-first verse is…"). Node `2.22` continues the exegesis of
homage 21's closing two lines (a dispute over whether they add a twenty-second item or
complete the count of twenty-one) and so joins `tara-21` rather than opening a new slot. Node
`3` (བསྟོད་པའི་ཕན་ཡོན) is a single undivided block running from the root text's benefit-teaching
through the commentator's own closing dedication and colophon — the tree gives it no finer
division, so the whole node is routed to `benefits`. Tārā's origin narrative sits inside
homage 1 (`2.1`) rather than as its own section, so `origin` is silent.
