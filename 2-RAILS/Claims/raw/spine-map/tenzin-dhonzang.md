**Structure of this commentary.** A six-part work announced in its own front matter (c-0-6):
a life-story of Tārā (node `1`), a history of how the tantra arose in Jambudvīpa (node `2`),
an undivided word-by-word doctrinal analysis of the root text's opening invocation "Oṃ,
homage to Ārya Tārā" (node `3`, 106 claims with no sub-headings in the tree or the source),
the explanation of the twenty-one homage verses themselves (node `4`), a brief statement of
benefits (node `5`), and an explanation of the mantra-garland commentary (node `6`). Node `4`
divides into twenty-one ordinal-titled children `4.1`–`4.21` (དང་པོ་… "first" through ཉེར་གཅིག་པ་…
"twenty-first") in exact one-to-one correspondence with the root text's `^1-1`–`^1-21`; each
child's own second claim quotes its root-verse line verbatim (e.g. `4.1`'s c-4-1-2 quotes
"གེ་སར་བྱེ་བ་ལས་ནི་བྱུང་མ" = root `^1-1`, and `4.21`'s c-4-21-2 quotes "འཇོམས་པ་ཏུ་རེ་རབ་མཆོག་ཉིད་མ" =
root `^1-21`), confirming the correspondence beyond the ordinal titles alone. Nodes `5` and
`6` are announcement-only: the source file ends mid-window of `4.21` at block `^0-291`, before
either section's own text begins, so neither holds any claims. Node `4`'s own single direct
claim (c-4-0-1, a transitional heading-echo) cannot be swept into any one `tara-NN` slot
without also sweeping its twenty-one children into the same row, so it is routed by claim
instead; the front matter (node `0`) is likewise a single flat bucket mixing the six-part
announcement with unrelated title/author/subject content, and since claim-level routing can
split node `0`'s claims apart, the whole node stays in the Unmapped nodes table.
