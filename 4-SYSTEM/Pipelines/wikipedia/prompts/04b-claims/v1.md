You are converting extracted commentary passages about the Tibetan term "$term" into an
**atomic claims table**. This table becomes the only input the drafting stages may use — the
passages themselves are never shown to the drafter again — so every fact the article will
state must exist here as a claim, and nothing else may.

## Rules

1. **One verifiable fact per claim, written in Tibetan in your own words.** Never reuse a
   source's sentence structure or distinctive phrasing. Split compound statements into
   separate claims.
2. **These glossary terms must appear verbatim, never paraphrased:** $glossary
3. **Every claim lists the passages that support it** (`passage_indices`, indices into the
   supplied passage list). A claim with no supporting passage must not exist. If two passages
   support the same claim, one claim, both indices.
4. **Conflicting positions get one claim each — never merge them into a compromise no source
   states.** Mark both `contested`.
5. **Claim type** — one of: `consensus` · `majority-with-dissent` · `school-position` ·
   `single-commentator`. Weight by authority and response, not headcount: a commentator who
   is the sole representative of his school in this corpus is a `school-position`, never
   `single-commentator`.
6. **Reception** — who cites this position, who refutes it (name the source texts, dgag lan
   and response literature included), or `unengaged`. A position that drew rebuttals has
   proven weight.
7. **School** — the commentator's school (from the source registry when given; leave ""
   rather than guess).
8. **Forbidden: synthesis.** No claim may require two sources combined to reach a conclusion
   neither states alone. Record temptations in `forbidden_syntheses` instead of creating the
   claim.
9. **Flag claims resting only on a copyrighted source** (`copyright_only: true` — readers can
   verify these only via a BDRC/WeBuddhist link, so the drafter must keep them attributed).
10. Add nothing from your own knowledge. Every claim traces to its passages.

## Output

JSON only:

```
{
 "term": "...",
 "claims": [
  {
   "id": "C1",
   "claim_bo": "…one fact, own words, Tibetan…",
   "passage_indices": [0, 3],
   "claim_type": "consensus | majority-with-dissent | school-position | single-commentator",
   "school": "…or empty…",
   "reception": "cited by … / refuted by … / unengaged",
   "contested": false,
   "copyright_only": false
  }
 ],
 "forbidden_syntheses": ["…each temptation you refused, one line…"]
}
```
