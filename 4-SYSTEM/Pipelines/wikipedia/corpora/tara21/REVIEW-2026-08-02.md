## The cross-model audit — what the review actually caught

STATE.md's rule: *never report a same-model audit as independent.* The sandbox audit was
claude-sonnet-5 judging claude-sonnet-5. Re-auditing locally with **gemini-3.5-flash**
(`kwiki audit`) flipped two of the three "publish, no findings" verdicts:

**Round 1 — 5 blocking findings, adjudicated by hand against claims.json:**

| # | term | finding | adjudication |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | སྒྲོལ་མ | lead says **many** scholars agree; claim 0 says **three** (མཁས་པ་མི་འདྲ་བ་གསུམ) | **genuine** — consensus exaggeration; the body section even had it right |
| 2 | སྒྲོལ་མ | "and none dispute it" (སུས་ཀྱང་མ་བསྙོན་པའོ) beyond the claim | **borderline** — claim 0's `reception` metadata does say "none contests it"; the auditor reads `claim_bo` only. Tightened to claim wording anyway; audit-prompt refinement noted |
| 3 | སྒྲོལ་མ | "a name for **each** verse" where claims support verses 2, 11, 17 only | **genuine** — overgeneralization |
| 4 | སྡུག་བསྔལ | lead said མཚན་ཉིད (defining characteristic); claim 0 is about the **name's meaning** (མཚན་དོན) | **genuine** — real semantic shift in technical vocabulary |

**Fix pass** (recorded in each article's `model.json` under `fix_passes`): six surgical

**Round 3 + final.** སྒྲོལ་མ and འཇིག་རྟེན་གསུམ: publish, 0 findings. སྡུག་བསྔལ drew one
last borderline objection to the lead's connective ("based on the meaning of the word
