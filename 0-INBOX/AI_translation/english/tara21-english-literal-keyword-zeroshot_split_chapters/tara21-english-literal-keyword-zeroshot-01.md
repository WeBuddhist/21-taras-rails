# Praise to Tara with Twenty-One Homages


## Title ^I-0

In the language of India: Namaḥ Tāre Ekaviṃśati Stotra Guṇahitasāka. In the language of Tibet: Homage to Tara, the Praise with Twenty-One, together with the Benefits. Homage to the Noble Lady, the Venerable Tara. ^I-1


## 1. The Twenty-One Homages ^1-0

Homage Tara swift heroine
Eyes like a flash of lightning in an instant
From the opening of the stamen of the water-born face
Of the protector of the three worlds arisen ^1-1

Homage to her whose face is like one hundred
Full autumn moons gathered together,
Blazing with the highly brilliant light
Of assemblies of thousands of stars. ^1-2

Homage to her whose hand is completely adorned
With a water-born lotus of golden blue,
She whose range of activity is giving, diligence, asceticism, pacification,
Patience, and meditative concentration itself. ^1-3

Homage to the crown protrusion of the Tathagata,
Who practices infinite victory,
Who has attained every single perfection,
Thoroughly relied upon by the sons of the Victorious One. ^1-4

Homage TUTTARA and HUM syllables,
Filling desire and directions and space,
Treading the seven worlds with her feet,
Able to summon without exception. ^1-5

Homage to her worshiped by Indra, Agni, Brahma,
Vayu, and the various Ishvaras,
And praised in her presence by the hosts of
Bhutas, vetalas, gandharvas, and yakshas. ^1-6

Homage to her who with the syllables TRAD and PHAT
Completely destroys the wheel-devices of adversaries,
Suppressing with feet retracted on the right and extended on the left,
Blazing intensely with a swirling mass of blazing fire. ^1-7

Homage TURE, great female terrifier,
Conqueror of the heroes of maras,
Making the water-born face possess frowning wrinkles,
Slayer of all enemies without exception. ^1-8

Homage to her whose fingers, in the mudra symbolizing the Three Jewels,
Beautifully adorn her heart,
Adorned with the wheel of all directions without exception,
Who agitates with masses of her own light. ^1-9

Homage to her whose brilliant, exceedingly joyful
Crown-ornament spreads garlands of light,
Who, with her laughing, supreme laugh of TUTTARA,
Subdues maras and the world. ^1-10

Homage to her who is able to summon all
The assemblies of guardians of the earth,
Who by the letter HUM moving with wrathful frown,
Thoroughly liberates from all poverty. ^1-11

Homage, [she whose] head is adorned with the crescent moon,
All [her] ornaments are exceedingly blazing,
From Amitabha in the midst of [her] matted hair,
Constantly making exceeding light. ^1-12

Homage to her who dwells within the center of a blazing garland
Like the fire of the end of the eon,
With right leg extended and left bent, surrounded with joy on all sides,
Who thoroughly destroys the host of enemies. ^1-13

Homage to her who with the palm of her hand upon the earth's surface
Strikes and with her foot stamps,
Who making a frowning grimace, with the letter HUM
Shatters the seven underworld levels. ^1-14

Homage to the blissful, virtuous, and peaceful one,
She whose sphere of activity is peace beyond sorrow,
By being perfectly endowed with SVAHA and OM,
She who is the very destroyer of great negativity. ^1-15

Homage to her who is surrounded by the completely joyful,
Who thoroughly destroys the bodies of enemies,
Who arranges the speech of the ten syllables,
And is herself the lamp arising from the knowledge-HUM. ^1-16

Homage, by stamping with the feet of TURE,
She who is the very seed-syllable in the form of HUM,
Meru, Mandara, and Vindhya,
She who causes the three worlds to tremble! ^1-17

Homage to her who holds in her hand the deer-marked
Which has the form of the celestial ocean;
By pronouncing two TARAs and the syllable PHAT,
She completely dispels all poisons without exception. ^1-18

Homage to the King of the assemblies of gods,
Who is attended by gods and kinnaras,
With the splendor of joyful armor from all sides,
Who dispels conflicts and bad dreams. ^1-19

Homage to her whose two eyes are like the fully expanded sun and moon,
Shedding a light that is exceedingly brilliant,
By uttering HARA twice and TUTTARA,
Dispersing the most extremely fierce infectious diseases. ^1-20

Homage to her endowed with the perfect power of peace,
By the arrangement of those three realities,
Destroying the hosts of demons, zombies, and yakshas,
She who is the indeed supreme TURE. ^1-21


## Benefits of the Praise ^a-0

With this praise by the root mantra,
And the twenty-one homages,
By any intelligent one who is endowed with perfect devotion
To the Goddess, by reciting [them] thoroughly, ^a-1

Having arisen at dusk and dawn,
By remembering, she completely bestows all fearlessness,
Completely pacifying all negative actions,
And utterly destroying all lower realms. ^a-2

By seventy million Conquerors
Quickly empowerment will be conferred, and
Attaining greatness even more than this,
One goes to that ultimate state of Buddha. ^a-3

That poison, fierce and great,
Whether abiding in the stable or also moving,
Even by eating and indeed drinking,
By remembering, obtains complete elimination. ^a-4

By demons and infectious diseases and poisons afflicted,
The assembly of sufferings is completely eliminated,
For other sentient beings as well,
If recited clearly two, three, [or] seven [times]. ^a-5

By those desiring a son, a son will be obtained, and
By those desiring wealth, wealth itself will be obtained.
All desires will be obtained, and
Obstacles will not exist and will be individually destroyed. ^a-6

The praise to the Bhagavati Tara spoken by the completely perfected Buddha is completed. ^a-7
