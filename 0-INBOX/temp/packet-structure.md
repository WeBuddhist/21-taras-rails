**English:** "From the supreme site of Potala, born from a green syllable TĀṂ, by the light of the TĀṂ syllable liberating beings — Tārā, together with your retinue, please come. With the diadems of gods and asuras bowing to your lotus feet, liberator from all destitution, Mother Tārā, to you I prostrate."
