## Claims used but not quoted

Nearly every claim ID listed above under "Claim ID(s) drawn on" that is not paired with a quotation
entered the article as paraphrased consensus/unique/divergence content, cited via `<ref>` without
quotation marks, per Rule 5 (consensus backbone cited to representative commentaries; unique material
attributed by name). This includes, notably: the full 10–15-commentary consensus attestation lists for
the Tārā/Swift/Heroine etymologies (§1 of `tara-01.md`), the wisdom-eye simile (§3), the origin
narrative (§4), and the three-worlds identification (§5) — the article cites a representative subset
(4–12 commentaries per fact, per Rule 5) rather than every attesting commentary, since the article is
prose, not a citation dump. The doctrinal quality-mapping schemas (§11: anon-trinle-char, taranatha,
tenzin-dhonzang) and the practice instructions (§12) are drawn on only lightly, in the
`ཕྲིན་ལས་དང་ནུས་མཐུ།` section, and not exhaustively — this was a scope decision to keep the article's
length proportionate to a homage-1 profile rather than reproduce every facet on `tara-01.md`.

Facets from `tara-01.md` **not** incorporated into the article at all: §2 (the grammatical "ma"-suffix
note), §7 (textual attributions/translator-variant citations, beyond the "dpal mo" root-verse variant
used in the divergence section), §9 (palden-sherab's fourfold outer/inner/secret/ultimate hermeneutical
scheme), §10's path-completion etymology is used but its full four-layer structure from §9 is not
reproduced, and §12 (practice instructions) is only glancingly present. These are legitimate editorial
omissions for space/focus, not unresolved attestations — a future revision could add a
`སྒྲུབ་པའི་མན་ངག` or similar section if the team wants §9/§12 represented.
