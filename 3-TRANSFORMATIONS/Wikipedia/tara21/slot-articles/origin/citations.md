# Citations — origin

## Scope decision: tara-01 material NOT pulled in

This is one of the skill's two documented multi-topic cases ("`origin` (optionally with the origin
material in `tara-01`) → the article on Tārā the deity"). Before drafting, `2-RAILS/Claims/tara-01.md`
and its published article at
`3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Wikipedia/tara21/slot-articles/tara-01/article.wiki` were read in full to decide
whether tara-01's own origin material belonged here too.

**Finding: tara-01 carries a *different* origin narrative, already fully published, and `origin.md`
itself already routes it away from this slot.** `tara-01.md` §4 ("Origin narrative: born from the
tears and lotus of the protector of the three worlds") is attested by **all fifteen** of its
contributing commentaries and is tied directly to the root verse's own fourth line
(`འཇིག་རྟེན་གསུམ་མགོན་ཆུ་སྐྱེས་ཞལ་གྱི། གེ་སར་ཕྱེ་བ་ལས་ནི་བྱུང་མ།`) — Avalokiteśvara weeping at
saṃsāra's inexhaustibility, a lotus growing from his tears, Tārā arising from its pollen. That
narrative is already drafted, cited, and quoted in full in `tara-01/article.wiki`'s own
`== ལོ་རྒྱུས། ==` section (15 attesting commentaries, 1 direct quotation, PASS-verified in that
article's own citations.md).

`origin.md`'s own "Further Avalokiteśvara-derived origin accounts" section explicitly says why this
material does *not* recur here: "the packet's twelve silent commentaries each report, in their own
silence entries, that they carry a similar Avalokiteśvara-derived origin account... but route it
inside their own homage-1 node rather than into this slot" (`origin.md` line ~141). This is the
vault's own prior editorial/spine-mapping decision, not a gap this run needed to fill. Re-drafting
that narrative into this article would (a) duplicate an already-published, already-verified section
word-for-word in substance, and (b) contradict the packet's own documented routing logic.

What `origin.md` *does* carry, and what this article uses instead, is a **structurally separate**
narrative: the princess Yeshe Dawa / Jñānacandrā and Buddha Dundhubhisvara (Drumsound) story, attested
by three commentaries (`sungrab-tulku`, `tenzin-dhonzang`, `tsultrim-namdak`) as a *dedicated,
top-level* history section outside any homage-1 numbering — plus a handful of further origin-adjacent
facets (two more Avalokiteśvara-derived accounts unique to `lobsang-dawa` and `tsultrim-namdak`
specifically, `sungrab-tulku`'s unique recitation-benefit coda, and `tenzin-dhonzang`'s unique account
of the tantra's transmission into Jambudvipa). This is genuinely distinct content, not a duplicate of
tara-01's tears/lotus story, so it is exactly what belongs in a standalone origin article.

**Resolution:** tara-01 material is not incorporated into `article.wiki`. Instead, `tara-01` is added to
`== འབྲེལ་ཡོད་ཤོག་ངོས། ==` as a related page, so a reader following the origin article to the
homage-1 profile finds the complementary tears/lotus narrative there.

## Facets from origin.md not incorporated into the article

transparency):

  vow (unique elaboration on the reply to the monks' urging).
- `tsultrim-namdak:c-2-1-1-8`'s "forgoes food every morning and evening" practice detail (the samādhi
  quotation itself is used; this specific elaboration is not).
- `tsultrim-namdak:c-2-1-1-13` — the Bhagavān instructing Mañjuśrī to hold the praise in mind and
  uttering the dhāraṇī-mantra (closes the "tears" episode; omitted as beyond origin-narrative scope).
- `tenzin-dhonzang:c-1-9` (Mount Malaya verse), `c-2-2` (three bodhisattvas taught tantra classes),
  `c-2-3`–`c-2-5` (siddhi attainment claims), `c-2-8`–`c-2-10` (quoted homage verse + "Khadiravani
  Tārā, chief of all Tārās" identification — the immediate lead-in to the c-2-11 divergence, which
  *is* used), `c-2-13`–`c-2-14` (no-contradiction-with-one-continuum doctrine, scriptural basis),
  `c-2-19`–`c-2-20` (second reductio + scriptural proof, continuing the teacher/compiler argument that
  is otherwise summarized), `c-2-21`–`c-2-22` (correspondence list and closing dedication verse) — all
  left out to keep the "transmission into Jambudvipa" subsection a proportionate summary rather than a
  full reproduction of `tenzin-dhonzang`'s own extended doctrinal excursus.

None of these omissions affect the article's claim-only sourcing; they are simply facets a future
revision could add if a reviewer wants fuller coverage of tenzin-dhonzang's transmission material.
