## Title convention

**Series prefix.** Every one of the 21 slot articles is titled སྒྲོལ་མ་<epithet>། — the prefix goes at the front without exception, so the whole series sorts and prefix-searches together on the wiki. Where the attested epithet is itself of the form X་གྱི་སྒྲོལ་མ, it is normalised to སྒྲོལ་མ་X། — the move already used live for tara-03 (གསེར་མདོག་ཅན་གྱི་སྒྲོལ་མ → སྒྲོལ་མ་གསེར་མདོག་ཅན།). Honorifics (རྗེ་བཙུན་མ་, རྗེ་བཙུན་) and any ལྷ་མོ་ element are stripped. Nothing is ever added: no syllable appears in a title that is not in the attested epithet.

**Where the epithet comes from.** The epithet must be one attested in the commentaries and named in the article's own མཚན་གྱི་ངེས་ཚིག section — never invented, never taken from parametric knowledge. Where the commentaries disagree on the name (they do for most slots), the choice among attested candidates is a human editorial decision, recorded per slot below.

**Choosing among competing names.** Where the commentaries name a slot differently — they do for most of them — the epithet is chosen by, in order: (1) widest attestation, the name given by the most commentaries; (2) on a tie, the name the article's own lead sentence or root verse already carries, so that title and article agree; (3) still tied, the form that reads as a name rather than as a description of the deity's activity, preferring the shorter and the ...མ ending the series uses. Every chosen epithet was checked to appear verbatim in its own article before being used. Recorded per slot as `decision_basis`; the rejected candidates are kept below so the choice stays auditable and the divergence itself is never lost — it remains documented in each article's མཚན་གྱི་ངེས་ཚིག section, which the title does not overwrite.

**Lead term stays in sync.** Each article's opening bolded term is kept identical to its title (minus the final shad, plus a tsheg inside the bold, per the live tara-01 form '''སྒྲོལ་མ་མྱུར་མ་དཔའ་མོ་'''ནི་). Updated for all 18 unpublished slots on 2026-08-21. tara-02 is the one live article where the two still differ.

**Subject articles.** Subject articles (origin, structure-benefits) take a plain descriptive Tibetan title; the སྒྲོལ་མ་ prefix rule does not apply to them.

**Do not auto-derive from the lead term.** The title is NOT auto-derivable from the article's first bolded term. In most slot articles that term is the opening line of the root verse (ཕྱག་འཚལ་…), not a name; in tara-02 the published title adds the སྒྲོལ་མ་ prefix the source file lacks. Any tooling that derives a title from the lead bold term must read this file instead.

### Why each contested slot went the way it did

- **tara-05** → སྒྲོལ་མ་ཧཱུཾ་སྒྲ་སྒྲོག་མ། — 3–3 split. Taken as the form that is a name rather than a name-plus-function-plus-ལྷ་མོ compound, and that carries the ...མ ending the rest of the series uses.
- **tara-06** → སྒྲོལ་མ་གདོན་རིགས་རྣམ་པར་འཇོམས་པ་འཇིགས་བྱེད་ཆེན་མོ། — Four commentaries against three. Kept at full attested length rather than shortened to འཇིགས་བྱེད་ཆེན་མོ, which would sit one syllable from tara-08's སྒྲོལ་མ་འཇིགས་པ་ཆེན་མོ། and confuse readers. Longest title in the series.
- **tara-07** → སྒྲོལ་མ་གཞན་གྱིས་མི་ཐུབ་མ། — The three-commentary name runs to eleven syllables; གཞན་གྱིས་མི་ཐུབ་མ is its verbatim tail and is the element the rival གཞན་...འཇོམས་མ readings also share, so it is the one name every bloc supports.
- **tara-09** → སྒྲོལ་མ་སེང་ལྡེང་ནགས། — Attested as སེང་ལྡེང་ནགས་ཀྱི་སྒྲོལ་མ; normalised by the same move already used live for tara-03 (གསེར་མདོག་ཅན་གྱི་སྒྲོལ་མ → སྒྲོལ་མ་གསེར་མདོག་ཅན།).
- **tara-11** → སྒྲོལ་མ་ཕོངས་པ་ཀུན་སེལ། — Six commentaries, six names, no plurality. Falls to the name the article itself adopts — it states ཡིག་ཆ་འདིར་ཡང་མཚན་དེ་བཀོལ་ཡོད — which was already its lead term.
- **tara-13** → སྒྲོལ་མ་མེ་ལྟར་འབར་བ། — Three-way tie at two commentaries each. Falls to the reading that matches the root verse the article opens with (བསྐལ་པ་ཐ་མའི་མེ་ལྟར), so title and lead agree.
- **tara-17** → སྒྲོལ་མ་དཔག་མེད་གནོན་མ། — Three commentaries against two and two.

## Recording a new publication

**Slot articles:**
1. Move the slot from `planned:` to `published:` in [published.yaml](published.yaml) with its page ID and URL.
2. In that slot's `article.md` frontmatter, add `source_status: published`, `wiki_title`, `wiki_pageid`, `wiki_url`, `wiki_site`, `published_date`, and `published_registry: ../../published.yaml` (`status:` itself stays `draft` — it tracks the wikitext draft state, not the publish state; see tara-01/02/03/04… for the pattern).
3. Regenerate this view.

**Term articles:** same as above, but add the entry under `term_articles:` (keyed by `term:`, not `slot:`) instead of `published:`/`planned:`.
