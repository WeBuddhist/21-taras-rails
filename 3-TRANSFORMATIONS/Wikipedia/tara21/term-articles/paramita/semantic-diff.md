## Reverted drift

Gemini systematically upgraded the plain verb ངོས་འཛིན་**བྱས/བྱེད** ("identifies") to the honorific ངོས་འཛིན་**མཛད** in three places — an unattested honorific elevation of register for a commentator's/commentaries' action, not present in the source prose. This is the same category of drift the skill's known pattern flags for honorific *titles* before personal names (Rule 8): an honorific not licensed by human curation must never be introduced by the polish pass, whether attached to a name or to a verb describing a named/collective subject's action. All three were surgically reverted in `article.md` to the source's exact wording (delete/restore, no new phrasing):

1. Dbye-ba §2, Könchok Thabkhé clause: `ངོས་འཛིན་མཛད་ནས` → restored to `ངོས་འཛིན་བྱས་ནས`.
2. Shes-rab section, "many commentaries" clause: `ངོས་འཛིན་མཛད་དེ` → restored to `ངོས་འཛིན་བྱེད་དེ`.
3. Bsdus-don section, "some [commentaries]" clause: `ངོས་འཛིན་མཛད་ཅིང` → restored to `ངོས་འཛིན་བྱེད་ཅིང`.

(Note: a fourth instance of `མཛད` — in the Phyag-'tshal-bzhi-pa section, "Könchok Thabkhé...བརྟག་དཔྱོད་མཛད་དེ") was already present in the source article and was left untouched — it is not a polish-introduced honorific.)

`body-after.txt` was left untouched as the raw model record; only `article.md` was corrected.
