# Citations — amitabha (འོད་དཔག་མེད།) — v2 revision

Scope: unchanged from the v1 `citations.md` — this article covers Amitābha (འོད་དཔག་མེད།)
as he appears in this corpus: identification within the root text's twelfth homage
(crescent-moon crown-ornament verse), effects attributed to his radiating light, his own
domain (a "blissful realm," i.e. Sukhāvatī) and distinctness from Amitāyus, a lineage
connection some commentaries draw to Tārā's own family, and the completion-stage /
empowerment-correspondence readings the corpus gives him — as attested by all sixteen
commentaries per `2-RAILS/Claims/amitabha.md`. This is a **Mode B register revision**: no
claim ID was re-derived from `2-RAILS/Claims/` or the raw tree-guided files, and no claim
ID appears below that did not already appear, resolved and PASS-verified, in the v1
`citations.md`. The rewrite converts the consensus identification facet and the effects
facet to wikivoice, cuts the quotation count from 34 to 2, and caps in-article refs per
statement at 3 — the divergence section (`གཞུང་ལུགས་སོ་སོའི་བཤད་པ།`) keeps every position
attributed by name (Rule 5), converting its quotations to attributed paraphrase since the
2-quotation budget was spent on the root verse and the lineage dedication verse.
