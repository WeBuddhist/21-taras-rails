# Citations — amitabha (འོད་དཔག་མེད།)

Scope: this article covers *Amitābha* (འོད་དཔག་མེད།) as he appears in this corpus —
identification within the root text's twelfth homage (the crescent-moon
crown-ornament verse, where Amitābha, not Vairocana, is named as dwelling amid
Tārā's matted locks), the effects attributed to his radiating light, his own
domain (a "blissful realm," i.e. Sukhāvatī) and his distinctness from the
separately-named figure Amitāyus, a lineage connection some commentaries draw
between Tārā's own family and Amitābha, and every completion-stage or
empowerment-correspondence reading the corpus gives him — as attested by all
sixteen commentaries per `2-RAILS/Claims/amitabha.md`. It deliberately excludes
material that belongs to the sibling topics `ushnisha.md` (a different verse,
the fourth homage, concerning Uṣṇīṣavijayā and Vairocana) and `tathagata.md`
(the generic epithet "Tathāgata" as such), even where a single claim's own
བོད་ཡིག happens to use both "Amitābha" and "Tathāgata" together
(`taranatha:c-12-7`).
