# Citations — tara-mantra

`gendun-gyatso` and `lobsang-dawa` are listed in `tara-mantra.md`'s `sources:` frontmatter but were not
drawn on in this article — a scope decision (see Warnings), not an extraction failure. Both raw files
exist and were available; this article simply did not need their specific attestations given the
representative-citation approach mandated by Adaptation 1 (see Warnings, "Selective citation"). This
scope decision predates today's Mode B revision and was carried forward unchanged from v1.
