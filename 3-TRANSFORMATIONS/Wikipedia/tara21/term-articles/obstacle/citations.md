# Citations — obstacle (བགེགས)

Scope: this article covers the two facets of `2-RAILS/Claims/obstacle.md` that are
genuinely distinct from the corpus's other bgegs-related material — (1) a root verse
(the "fourteenth" homage, in several commentaries' own numbering) whose destroyer-class
is variously named, including two commentaries who name it explicitly as
"dam-sri-bgegs"; and (2) a recurring gloss identifying "'byung po" (bhūta) — one of the
classes said to worship Tārā — specifically as "the class of bgegs." A short activity
section adds `tenzin-dhonzang`'s ransom (*glud*) practice and its recurring "chief
activity" framing for bgegs. The great majority of the topic page's other bgegs
occurrences (the closing benefits verse's stock "obstacles destroyed" line, generic
dgra-bgegs/gdon-bgegs idioms, and content already covered by `gdon.md` or `mara.md`)
are deliberately **not** repeated here — see `2-RAILS/Claims/obstacle.md`'s own "Claims
reviewed, not separately cited" section for that material and its disposition.
