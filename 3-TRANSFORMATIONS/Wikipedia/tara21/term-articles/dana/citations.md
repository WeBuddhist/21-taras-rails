# Citations — dāna (སྦྱིན་པ)

Scope: this article covers *dāna* (སྦྱིན་པ, generosity) as a doctrinal/practice
category — the first of the six pāramitās — as attested by all sixteen
commentaries surveyed in `2-RAILS/Claims/dana.md`. It deliberately excludes
two unrelated senses of the same syllables also found in the corpus: the
iconographic mudrā name མཆོག་སྦྱིན (*mchog-sbyin*, "supreme-giving"/*varada*
mudrā) and the generic verb "to grant/bestow" describing Tārā's own activity
(granting fearlessness, blessings, siddhis). Both exclusions are recorded on
the consolidated page's own "Claims reviewed, not separately cited" section
and are not drawn on here.
