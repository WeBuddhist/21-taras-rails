# Citations — dāna (སྦྱིན་པ)

**Mode B revision (2026-08-20).** Register-only rewrite of the v1 article
(`wiki-article-from-claims`) into v2 register. No claim ID, quotation, or `Cite:`
target was re-derived; every attestation below was already verified in v1's
`citations.md`. Scope is unchanged from v1: *dāna* (སྦྱིན་པ, generosity) as the first
of the six pāramitās, as attested by all sixteen surveyed commentaries, excluding the
iconographic *varada*-mudrā sense and the generic verb "to grant/bestow."

## What changed

- **Quotation budget applied (Rule 6).** v1 carried 10 verbatim quotations; v2 keeps
  **2**: the root verse itself ("སྦྱིན་པ་བརྩོན་འགྲུས་དཀའ་ཐུབ་ཞི་བ། ...", `tenzin-dhonzang:c-4-3-2`)
  in the lead — kept because it is the root-text passage all sixteen commentaries
  independently gloss — and the three-fold classification ("སྦྱིན་པ་ལ་ཟང་ཟིང་གི་སྦྱིན་པ་
  དང་། ...", `tenzin-dhonzang:c-4-3-6`) under `དབྱེ་བ།`, kept because it is a fixed
  doctrinal formula whose precise three-part wording is itself the content being
  reported. The other 8 v1 quotations were converted to paraphrase, grounded in the
  same claim's recorded meaning; no new claim content was introduced.
- **Wikivoice conversion (Rule 5).** The lead's two backbone sentences (dāna as first
  pāramitā / root-verse attestation, and the golden-lotus imagery with the six
  pāramitās as Tārā's conduct) are now unattributed consensus prose, each citing 3
  representative refs instead of v1's 4 and 10 respectively (see citation-cap note
  below). The paramita-count variance and antidote-to-stinginess material in `དབྱེ་བ།`
  and `སེར་སྣའི་གཉེན་པོར་བཤད་པ།` stayed close to v1's own structure (already fairly
  attributed to distinct single/paired commentaries making distinct points) but is now
  connective wikivoice prose rather than quote-stitched sentences.
- **Attribution retained** for `drakpa-gyaltsen` (unique literal-meaning reading),
  `konchok-thabkhe` and `tsultrim-namdak` (distinct pāramita-count framings), and
  throughout `གཞུང་ལུགས་སོ་སོའི་བཤད་པ།` and `ལུང་གཞན་ལས་དྲངས་པའི་བཤད་པ།` for
  `taranatha`'s and `palden-sherab`'s hidden-meaning readings and `tenzin-dhonzang`'s
  canonical citations — each a genuinely distinctive single-commentary contribution,
  per Rule 5's attribution-for-unique-claims exception.
- **Citation cap (Rule 7) — the lead's two backbone sentences exceeded 3 refs in v1:**

    `palden-sherab`, `tsultrim-namdak`). v2 keeps 3 (`tenzin-dhonzang`, `palden-sherab`,
    `yama-sonam`); `tsultrim-namdak:c-2-1-2-1-10` is logged below.

    (`dharmabhadra`, `sungrab-tulku`, `gendun-drub`); the remaining 7 are logged below.
- **In-prose author names (Rule 17).** All in-prose names already used each
  commentary's `author_in_use` value in v1 (confirmed by frontmatter-only lookup of
  all sixteen raw claims files listed above). No fallback or warning needed.
