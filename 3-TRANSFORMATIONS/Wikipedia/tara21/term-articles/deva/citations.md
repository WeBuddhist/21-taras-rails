# Citations — deva (ལྷ)

Scope: this article covers ལྷ (deva/god) strictly as a *generic being-class* — its
place in the cosmological "three worlds" and the five/six/seven-fold rebirth scheme,
the special class of local nature-deities, the classificatory term "great worldly
gods," and the "deva-lake" poetic epithet for the moon — per `2-RAILS/Claims/deva.md`'s
own scope boundary. It deliberately excludes any content about a specific named deity
(Indra, Brahmā, Agni, Vāyu) or about Tārā herself (referred to throughout the corpus as
ལྷ་མོ, "the goddess"), which are the sibling topics `indra.md`, `brahma.md`, `agni.md`,
`vayu.md`, and the eventual Tārā deity article.

Of the sixteen commentaries the consolidated page draws on, this article draws
prose/quotations from nine: `anon-utpala`, `sungrab-tulku`, `tenga-tulku`, `taranatha`,
`tsultrim-namdak`, `tenzin-dhonzang`, `gendun-drub`, `konchok-thabkhe`, and
`palden-sherab`. The other seven (`anon-trinle-char`, `drakpa-gyaltsen`,
`gendun-gyatso`, `karma-maitri`, `lobsang-dawa`, `pema-namgyal`, `sangye-nyentrul`)
contributed corroborating attestations to the consolidated page's facets but were not
individually quoted in the article, per Rule 5 (due weight — consensus facets are
cited to a representative few, not to every corroborating commentary) — see "Claims
resolved but not incorporated" below.
