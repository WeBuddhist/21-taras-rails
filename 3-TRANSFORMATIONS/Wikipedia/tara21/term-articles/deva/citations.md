# Citations — deva (ལྷ)

**Mode B revision (2026-08-20).** Register-only rewrite of the v1 article
(`wiki-article-from-claims`) into v2 register. No claim ID, quotation, or `Cite:`
target was re-derived; every attestation below was already verified in v1's
`citations.md`. Scope is unchanged from v1: ལྷ (deva/god) as a generic being-class —
cosmological placement, nature-deities, the "great worldly gods" classificatory term,
and the "deva-lake" poetic epithet for the moon — excluding any specific named deity
and excluding Tārā herself (always ལྷ་མོ in this corpus).
