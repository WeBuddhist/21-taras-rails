# Citations — ཕཊ (phat)

Scope: this article covers ཕཊ specifically — its word-gloss, its function paired with
TRAD/TRAṬ at the "Destroyer of Others' Power" homage, the divergent readings
commentators give it (functional, phowa-based, completion-stage, alternative gloss),
the exegetical divergence over its exact placement in the poison-dispelling mantra, and
its attested use as a generic mantra-closing syllable in ritual formulas outside the
root text's own Tārā mantra. Per `2-RAILS/Claims/phat.md`'s own scope note, it
deliberately excludes the plain "TĀRA (twice) + PHAṬ dispels poison" mantra-recitation
content already covered by the sibling article `2-RAILS/Claims/tara-mantra.md` /
`3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Wikipedia/tara21/term-articles/tara-mantra/`, and does not restate
`mantra.md`'s or `hum-syllable.md`'s generic-syllable material.

`gendun-gyatso` is the one commentary among the 16 in the packet not cited in this
article: its sole ཕཊ-related claim (`c-1-18-2`) restates the plain poison-dispelling
mantra content already covered by `tara-mantra.md` and was logged on the consolidated
page under "Claims reviewed, not separately cited," never entering a facet this article
draws on (Rule 1: article statements trace only to claims actually cited on the
consolidated page's facets).
