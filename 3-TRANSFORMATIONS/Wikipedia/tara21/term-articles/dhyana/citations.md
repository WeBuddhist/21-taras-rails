# Citations — dhyāna (བསམ་གཏན)

**Mode B revision (2026-08-20).** Register-only rewrite of the v1 article
(`wiki-article-from-claims`) into v2 register. No claim ID, quotation, or `Cite:`
target was re-derived; every attestation below was already verified in v1's
`citations.md`. Scope is unchanged from v1: *dhyāna* (བསམ་གཏན, "concentration") as
attested across the sixteen tree-guided commentary extractions — its position among
the six pāramitās, its framing as antidote to specific mental faults, its
completion-stage/secret-meaning reading, its place in an extended ten-pāramitā
scheme, its threefold subdivision, canonical/scriptural elaborations, and broader
non-pāramitā uses of the same word.

## What changed

- **Quotation budget applied (Rule 6).** v1 carried 14 verbatim quotations; v2 keeps
  **2**: the root verse itself ("ཕྱག་འཚལ་གསེར་སྔོ་ཆུ་ནས་སྐྱེས་ཀྱི།། ...", `sungrab-tulku:c-6-2`)
  in the lead — kept because it is the root-text passage all sixteen commentaries
  independently gloss, sharing the exact wording with the sibling `dana` article's own
  root-verse quotation — and the canonical scriptural definition ("མི་སེམས་པ་ནི་བསམ་གཏན་
  ནོ།", `palden-sherab:c-3-1-3-4-2`, from the *Brahmaviśeṣacintīparipṛcchā*), kept
  because a formal sūtra-sourced definition's exact wording is itself the point. The
  other 12 v1 quotations were converted to paraphrase, grounded in the same claim's
  recorded meaning; no new claim content was introduced.
- **Wikivoice conversion (Rule 5)** is limited here relative to the sibling articles,
  because most of this article's content was already single- or paired-commentary
  material in v1 rather than broad consensus (its threefold subdivision, its
  extended ten-pāramitā placement, and its several completion-stage/hidden-meaning
  readings are each one or two commentaries' own distinctive elaboration). One
  exception, corrected in this pass: dhyāna's antidote framing ("concentration is
  the antidote to distraction") is a 3-commentary consensus on
  `2-RAILS/Claims/dhyana.md` (`sungrab-tulku:c-6-5`, `tenga-tulku:c-1-2-2-1-1-2-4`,

  wikivoice with the same `sungrab-tulku`/`tenga-tulku` refs attached, no claim ID
  added or removed. Tenzin Dhonzang's further elaboration into three named
