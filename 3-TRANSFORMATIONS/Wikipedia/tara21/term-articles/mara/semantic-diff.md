## Reverted drift

**Location:** first paragraph, second sentence — the quality ascribed to the first of the seven wrathful homages.

- **Source (article.md before polish):** `བདུད་དང་སྒྲིབ་གཉིས་སྤྱོད་པའི་ཡོན་ཏན་ཡིན་པར་བཤད་ཅིང་།` — "explained as the quality of **engaging with** (spyod pa) both mara and obscuration."
- **Gemini's output (body-after.txt, still on record):** `བདུད་དང་སྒྲིབ་གཉིས་སྤོང་བའི་ཡོན་ཏན་ཡིན་པར་བཤད་ཅིང་།` — "explained as the quality of **abandoning/relinquishing** (spong ba) both mara and obscuration."
- **Why this is drift:** spyod pa ("to engage in/practice") and spong ba ("to abandon/relinquish") are near-homographic but semantically opposite verbs describing what quality the homage ascribes with respect to mara and obscuration. This is exactly the pattern-(f) risk flagged in the task brief: a near-homograph swap changing the whole term's meaning. Regardless of which reading is doctrinally more natural, Rule 1 requires the source article to be treated as the complete, frozen factual contract — this skill does not adjudicate what the commentaries "should" say.
- **Remedy applied:** surgical reversion (Rule 8a). `article.md` was edited to restore the source's exact wording (སྤྱོད་པའི). `body-after.txt` was left untouched as the raw model record, per the rule.
