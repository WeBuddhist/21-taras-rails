iconographic/symbolic topic attested across the corpus, not one of the 21 Tārā deity slots. No
attested pure etymology of the Tibetan word *'khor lo* itself exists in the consolidated page's
claims, so `ངེས་ཚིག` is omitted (sections are a menu, per Rule 9 — a section is emitted only when
the source page attests material for it).

**Scope framing per the task's Wikipedia-status guidance:** bo.wikipedia already carries a stub
titled འཁོར་ལོ། (per the vault's Step 8 inventory; snapshot at
`3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Wikipedia/tara21/work/wiki-snapshots/`). This draft is scoped to *this text's own
commentarial treatment* of the wheel — how it appears across the Twenty-One Homages' iconography and
where the sixteen commentaries diverge — rather than re-deriving general pan-Buddhist dharma-wheel
symbolism. Whether/how this merges into the existing stub is a later human decision.
