# Citations — wheel (འཁོར་ལོ།)

**Revision note (Mode B, wiki-article-from-claims-v2).** This is a register-only rewrite of the
v1 article at the `source_article` path above. No claim ID has been introduced that was not
already present in the v1 `citations.md`'s reference map. No return was made to
`2-RAILS/Claims/wheel.md` or to the raw tree-guided files for content; the only frontmatter-only
lookup performed was for `author_in_use` (Rule 17), confirming all 16 raw claims files already
carry that key directly — no fallback or warning needed. The claim-resolution table, the
quotation-verification PASS records, and the scope framing below are otherwise unchanged from v1
and are carried forward as settled ground truth.

**Skeleton used:** the wikitext spec's §1 doctrinal-term skeleton (ངེས་ཚིག / མཚན་ཉིད། / དབྱེ་བ། /

`ངེས་ཚིག` remains omitted — no pure etymology claim exists on the consolidated page.

**Scope framing (unchanged from v1):** this draft is scoped to this text's own commentarial
treatment of the wheel — how it appears across the Twenty-One Homages' iconography and where the
sixteen commentaries diverge — rather than general pan-Buddhist dharma-wheel symbolism. A stub
already exists at bo.wikipedia title འཁོར་ལོ།; whether/how this merges into it is a later human
decision.
