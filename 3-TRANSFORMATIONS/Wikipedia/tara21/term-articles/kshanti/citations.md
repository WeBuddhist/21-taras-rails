# Citations — kshanti (བཟོད་པ)

Scope: this article covers *kṣānti* (བཟོད་པ, "patience"), one of the six
pāramitās, as attested in the twenty-one-praise commentarial corpus: its
appearance in the root verse's six-pāramitā list, its classification as
antidote to anger, an extended ten-pāramitā placement, two hidden-meaning
(sbas don) readings, an ultimate/non-referential (Madhyamaka) definition, a
threefold subdivision, one commentary's own labeled alternate reading, and —
kept clearly separated, per `2-RAILS/Claims/kshanti.md`'s own scope note — the
doctrinally adjacent but distinct "acceptance of the non-arising of
phenomena" (anutpattika-dharmakṣānti) narrated in Tārā's origin story. Nine of
the sixteen commentaries in the consolidated page are drawn on directly in
this article's prose (the remaining seven are cited on the consolidated page
for the same "six-pāramitā list" consensus facet but were not selected as one
of this article's 2–4 representative refs for that facet, per Rule 5's due-weight
guidance — see `2-RAILS/Claims/kshanti.md` for their citations).
