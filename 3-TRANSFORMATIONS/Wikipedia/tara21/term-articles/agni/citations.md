# Citations — agni (མེ་ལྷ)

Scope: this article covers Agni (མེ་ལྷ) as an independent minor deity within the
commentarial tradition on the Twenty-One Homages to Tārā — his recurring appearance
among the worldly gods who worship Tārā in the fivefold homage-verse, his epithet as
"sage" / "chief of sages" (drang srong), his place among a fuller set of ten
directional guardians summoned into Tārā's service in two commentaries, and a tantric
hidden-meaning identification with the fire element — as attested by all sixteen
commentaries surveyed in `2-RAILS/Claims/agni.md`. Not every one of that page's 33
consulted claims is individually quoted or paraphrased below (the "worshipper-list"
facet alone carries 22 near-duplicate attestations of the same underlying fact); the
article draws a representative, due-weight subset per Rule 5 of
`wiki-article-from-claims`, while every commentary that contributed to the
consolidated page is cited at least once in the article.
