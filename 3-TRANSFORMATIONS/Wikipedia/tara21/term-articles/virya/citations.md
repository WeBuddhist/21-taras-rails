as attested by the fifteen commentaries that discuss it in `2-RAILS/Claims/virya.md`
(`gendun-gyatso` is silent on this term and is not cited). Content follows the
consolidated page's facet structure: identity/definition, the six-pāramitā identification
at root verse 3 (tara-03) with its causal elaboration, internal classification, the
laziness-antidote relationship, the extended ten-pāramitā enumeration, tantric/
completion-stage usage, and each commentary's own distinctive framing (count divergence,
blessing-cause, teaching-upholding, ultimate/negation definition, hidden meaning).
