# Citations — སྭཱ་ཧཱ (svaha)

Scope: this article covers SVĀHĀ specifically — its attested word-glosses ("peace"/
pacifying-sin-increasing-virtue; "homage to you"; an activity-ending classification), a
doctrinal "indestructible speech-sound" reading, its use as a metonymic name for the
whole ten-syllable mantra, and its attested appearance as a generic mantra-closing
syllable in ritual formulas unrelated to the root text's own Tārā mantra. Per
`2-RAILS/Claims/svaha.md`'s own scope note, it deliberately excludes the fifteenth
homage's own root-verse "genuinely endowed with SVĀHĀ and OṂ, she destroys great sin"
content — that is already the per-verse spine page `2-RAILS/Claims/tara-15.md`'s own
Consensus facet — and does not restate `tara-mantra.md`'s, `mantra.md`'s,
`hum-syllable.md`'s, or `phat.md`'s material.

Only 4 of the 16 commentaries consulted by `2-RAILS/Claims/svaha.md` land a citation in
this article (`pema-namgyal`, `sungrab-tulku`, `taranatha`, `tsultrim-namdak`); the
other 12 each contributed at least one claim to the consolidated page's gather, but
every one of those claims was logged there under "Claims reviewed, not separately
cited" — plain root-verse or mantra-formula content whose interpretive territory
belongs to a sibling page, not to SVĀHĀ's own distinctive treatment (per Rule 1: article
statements trace only to claims actually cited on the consolidated page's facets).
