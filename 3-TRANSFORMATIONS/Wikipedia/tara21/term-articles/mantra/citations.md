# Citations — mantra (སྔགས)

Scope: this article covers *mantra* (སྔགས) as a generic Vajrayana category — etymology,
classification, the term's function within this text's own structure, powers, and
placement in practice — as attested by the twelve commentaries that discuss it (per
`2-RAILS/Claims/mantra.md`'s own Signal-A routing). It deliberately excludes the
syllable-by-syllable decoding of the text's own specific formula *oṃ tāre tuttāre ture
svāhā*, which is the sibling topic `2-RAILS/Claims/tara-mantra.md` and a separate article.
