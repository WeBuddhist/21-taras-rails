# Citations — buddha (སངས་རྒྱས)

Scope: this article covers *Buddha* (སངས་རྒྱས) as the plain, literal doctrinal
term/category — as the practitioner's soteriological goal, the doctrinal basis
of that goal (Prajñāpāramitā-dependence, the six perfections), its place in
the refuge formula and the four types of noble offspring, and one
commentary's (Khenchen Palden Sherab's) distinct Atiyoga reading of the term
as a primordial rather than future nature — as attested by the eight
commentaries actually drawn on, per `2-RAILS/Claims/buddha.md`'s own facet
structure. It deliberately leaves out the sibling honorific epithets རྒྱལ་བ
("Victor") and དེ་བཞིན་གཤེགས་པ ("Tathāgata"), which are the separate articles
`conqueror` and `tathagata`, and the great majority of the consolidated
page's other facets (mother-of-buddhas, embodiment-of-activity, the origin
narratives, the Vairocana colophon attribution, the seven-branch liturgy,
and the long miscellaneous-claims list) — the article draws a focused
selection sufficient for a coherent term profile rather than exhausting
every attestation on the topic page.
