# Citations — buddha (སངས་རྒྱས)

**Mode B revision (2026-08-20).** This is a register-only rewrite of the v1 article
(`wiki-article-from-claims`) into v2 register (`wiki-article-from-claims-v2`), per the
skill's Mode B procedure. No claim ID, quotation, or `Cite:` target was re-derived or
newly introduced — every attestation below was already verified in the v1
`citations.md` and is carried forward unchanged. Only the prose register, quotation
count, and punctuation changed.

Scope (unchanged from v1): this article covers *Buddha* (སངས་རྒྱས) as the plain,
literal doctrinal term/category — as the practitioner's soteriological goal, the
doctrinal basis of that goal (Prajñāpāramitā-dependence, the six perfections), its
place in the refuge formula and the four types of noble offspring, and one
commentary's (Khenchen Palden Sherab's) distinct Atiyoga reading of the term as a
primordial rather than future nature — as attested by the eight commentaries actually
drawn on, per `2-RAILS/Claims/buddha.md`'s own facet structure. It deliberately leaves
out the sibling honorific epithets རྒྱལ་བ ("Victor") and དེ་བཞིན་གཤེགས་པ ("Tathāgata"),
which are the separate articles `conqueror` and `tathagata`, and the great majority of
the consolidated page's other facets — unchanged from v1's own scope note.
