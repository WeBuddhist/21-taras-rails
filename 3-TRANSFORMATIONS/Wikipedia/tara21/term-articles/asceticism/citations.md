# Citations — asceticism (དཀའ་ཐུབ།) — v2 revision

Scope: unchanged from the v1 `citations.md` — this article covers asceticism/austerity
(དཀའ་ཐུབ, tapas) as attested across `2-RAILS/Claims/asceticism.md`: the discipline-pāramitā
gloss of Tārā-3's root verse (consensus, with konchok-thabkhe's divergent five-term
parse), a doctrinal-classificatory sense (the "inner vehicles" of the nine-vehicle
system), two distinct completion-stage tantric hidden-meaning readings (lotus–vajra
union; the mantra syllable TUTTĀRE), tsultrim-namdak's divergent classification into the
"37 factors of enlightenment," and an iconographic sense ("in the form of an ascetic,"
identified with Amitābha in Tārā's locks). This is a **Mode B register revision**: no
claim ID was re-derived from `2-RAILS/Claims/` or the raw tree-guided files, and no claim
ID appears below that did not already appear, resolved and PASS-verified, in the v1
`citations.md`. The rewrite converts the twelve-commentary discipline-pāramitā backbone
to wikivoice, cuts the quotation count from 9 to 2, and caps in-article refs per statement
at 3 — both divergences (konchok-thabkhe's merge of terms; tsultrim-namdak's alternate
37-factors classification) keep their full attribution per Rule 5.
