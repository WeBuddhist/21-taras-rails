# Citations — asceticism (དཀའ་ཐུབ།)

Scope: this article covers *asceticism/austerity* (དཀའ་ཐུབ, tapas) as attested across
`2-RAILS/Claims/asceticism.md` — the discipline-pāramitā gloss of Tārā-3's root verse
(consensus, with konchok-thabkhe's divergent five-term parse), a doctrinal-classificatory
sense (the "inner vehicles" of the nine-vehicle system), two distinct completion-stage
("hidden meaning") tantric readings (lotus–vajra union; the mantra syllable TUTTĀRE), and
an iconographic sense ("in the form of an ascetic," identified with Amitābha in Tārā's
locks). Thirteen of the sixteen commentaries consulted on the consolidated page are cited
here; three (`anon-trinle-char`, `anon-utpala`, `sungrab-tulku`) attest only a verbatim
quotation of the root verse with no independent gloss and are not cited in this article
(per the consolidated page's "Claims reviewed, not separately cited").
