# Citations — tathagata (དེ་བཞིན་གཤེགས་པ)

**Revision note (2026-08-21, Mode B, `wiki-article-from-claims-v2`).** This file and the
sibling `article.md` were rewritten in place from the v1 draft (method
`wiki-article-from-claims`, dated 2026-08-14) to convert v1's claims-stitched register
into wikivoice prose, per the skill's Mode B procedure. No claim ID beyond what v1's own
citations.md already recorded was introduced. `2-RAILS/Claims/`, the raw tree-guided
files, and `1-SOURCES/` were not reopened for claim content — only their frontmatter was
read once, for `author_in_use` resolution (Rule 17). The reference map, "Claims used but
not quoted," "Unresolvable attestations," and "Verification" sections below are v1's own
work, carried forward unchanged (Mode B step 8) for every ref retained in the revised
article; only the "Full attestation beyond in-article refs" section and this note are new.

Scope: this article covers *Tathāgata* (དེ་བཞིན་གཤེགས་པ) as a generic buddha-epithet in
this corpus — its etymology, its dominant use in the root text's fourth homage verse
(the goddess as the Tathāgatas' crown-ornament / uṣṇīṣa), named individual buddhas
called "Tathāgata" (Vairocana, Amitābha, Dundhubhisvara/Drumsound), tantric
class-term uses, and one commentary's own completion-stage doctrinal reading — as
attested by all sixteen commentaries per `2-RAILS/Claims/tathagata.md`. It deliberately
excludes the parallel epithet རྒྱལ་བ ("Victor"), which is the sibling topic
`2-RAILS/Claims/conqueror.md` and its own article, even where the two notions occur in
the same commentarial sentence.
