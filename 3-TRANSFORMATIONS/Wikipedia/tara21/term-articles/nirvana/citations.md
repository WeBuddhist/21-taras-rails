# Citations — nirvana (མྱ་ངན་འདས།)

Scope: this article covers *nirvāṇa* (མྱ་ངན་འདས།) as it is treated across the corpus's
tree-guided claims extractions — the fourth term of the recurring "blissful, virtuous,
peaceful, [her domain is] nirvāṇa" epithet-quartet (fifteenth homage), the technical term
"non-abiding nirvāṇa," Tenzin Dhonzang's independent scholastic excursus distinguishing
natural nirvāṇa of the basis from nirvāṇa of the path, the internal divergences on how to
read the epithet-quartet, the three independent tantric completion-stage unpackings of the
term, and the doctrine that a buddha's passing into nirvāṇa is displayed rather than real —
per `2-RAILS/Claims/nirvana.md`. It deliberately excludes and only cross-references the
bodhisattva vow not to pass into nirvāṇa while beings suffer, which is `suffering.md`'s own
facet and is not re-argued here (see the rails page's own cross-reference note).
