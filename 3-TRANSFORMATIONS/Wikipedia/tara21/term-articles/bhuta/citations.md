# Citations — bhuta (འབྱུང་པོ)

Scope: this article covers *bhūta* (འབྱུང་པོ) as one member of the fourfold host
(bhūta, vetāla, gandharva, yakṣa) attested in the sixth homage of this praise —
its textual attestation, the four converging/diverging namings of its identity
found across the corpus, its tantric completion-stage equivalence, and a small
set of further activity/narrative mentions — as consolidated on
`2-RAILS/Claims/bhuta.md`. Per that page's own framing note, one identity
position (bhūta glossed as the class of bgegs/obstructing spirits) is a
cross-reference to `2-RAILS/Claims/obstacle.md`'s own prior finding rather than
independently re-derived; this article draws the same four claim IDs from the
consolidated page and presents them in the body without repeating obstacle.md's
own analysis verbatim.
