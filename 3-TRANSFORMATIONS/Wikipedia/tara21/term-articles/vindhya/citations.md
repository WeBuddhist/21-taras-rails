# Citations — vindhya (འབིགས་བྱེད)

Scope: this article covers *Vindhya* (འབིགས་བྱེད) as the third mountain named alongside
Meru and Mandara in the seventeenth homage of the *Twenty-One Praises to Tārā*, per
`2-RAILS/Claims/vindhya.md`. That consolidated page is itself framed as substantially a
cross-reference to `2-RAILS/Claims/meru.md` for the core naming/epithet material; this
article draws the same underlying claims directly from their raw tree-guided files (never
from `meru.md` itself, per the skill's fixed resolution chain), and gives independent full
weight to the two facets `meru.md` does not carry: the name-vs-verb grammatical divergence,
and the generation-stage sādhana visualization.
