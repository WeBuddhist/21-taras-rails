# Citations — eon (བསྐལ་པ)

Scope: this article covers *eon* (བསྐལ་པ / the attested orthographic variant བསྐལ་བ) as
attested across the corpus's commentarial treatment — principally the standard "fire at
the end of the aeon" simile applied to Tārā in the corpus's thirteenth homage, its
cosmological gloss, its doctrinal reading as her own wisdom, Palden Sherab's two
completion-stage readings, and every other attested use of the term (antiquity marker,
duration of meditative practice, hyperbolic unit in dedication/benefit-comparison
contexts, and a marker for the timing of scriptural transmission), per
`2-RAILS/Claims/eon.md`'s own facet structure. It deliberately does not draw on general
Abhidharma kalpa-cosmology (the four kalpas, the kalpa's length, etc.) because no claim
in the corpus's raw files attests that material — only what these sixteen commentaries
actually say about བསྐལ་པ in the course of glossing this specific praise text.

Twelve of the sixteen commentaries surveyed on the consolidated page are drawn on directly
in the article body (the article follows Rule 5: consensus facets are cited to 2–4
representative commentaries, not to all sixteen). The remaining four consulted
commentaries — `gendun-gyatso`, `tenga-tulku`, `lobsang-dawa`, `anon-utpala` — attest only
the base "Identity" facet (the standard fire-simile itself), which is already backed by
`gendun-drub` and `konchok-thabkhe` in the lead; they are not separately cited to keep the
article's citation load proportionate, per the same rule that governs `2-RAILS/Claims/mantra.md`'s
sibling article.
