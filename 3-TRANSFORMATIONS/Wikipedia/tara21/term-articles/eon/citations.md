# Citations — eon (བསྐལ་པ)

**Mode B revision.** This is a register-only rewrite of the v1 article (same topic,
same claim set). No claim ID appears in the revised article that was not already present
in the v1 `citations.md` reference map below. All twelve raw sources, all eighteen claim
IDs, all `Cite:` targets, and the original PASS verification are carried forward as
settled ground truth per the Mode B procedure — only the register (wikivoice vs.
attribution), the quotation budget, the citation cap, and the punctuation/naming
conventions changed.

Scope note (carried from v1, unchanged): this article covers *eon* (བসྐལ་པ / the
attested orthographic variant བსྐལ་བ) as attested across the corpus's commentarial
treatment — principally the standard "fire at the end of the aeon" simile applied to
Tārā in the corpus's thirteenth homage, its cosmological gloss, its doctrinal reading as
her own wisdom, Palden Sherab's two completion-stage readings, and every other attested
use of the term, per `2-RAILS/Claims/eon.md`'s own facet structure.
