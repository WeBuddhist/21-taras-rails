# Citations — yaksha (གནོད་སྦྱིན།)

Scope: this article covers *yakṣa* (གནོད་སྦྱིན) as an independent class of nature-spirit
being in this corpus's own commentarial treatment — its role in the verse-6 fourfold host,
divergent traditions on its chief/ruler, a completion-stage equivalence, its place among
the ten directional guardians, iconography, narrative episodes, and a granted siddhi — as
attested by the sixteen commentaries per `2-RAILS/Claims/yaksha.md`'s own gather. Per that
consolidated page's own framing, this article does **not** independently re-derive the
root-verse-21 gdon–ro-langs–gnod-sbyin triad (Ture's destruction of the three classes) —
that material is `2-RAILS/Claims/gdon.md`'s own territory and is left to a future
`gdon` article; this article's lead and etymology section instead draw on the
"radiance/luster-stealer" epithet facet, which is new synthesis not already covered as its
own theme on either prior page.
