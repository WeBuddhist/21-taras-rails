  `taranatha:c-11-3` from its inline refs; see *Full attestation beyond in-article
  refs* below.

  gap this revision's frontmatter lookup surfaced:**

    `(མིང་གཞན་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱ་མཚོ)` that is part of the formal `author` field in

    (`ཇོ་ནང་ཏཱ་ར་ནཱ་ཐ`) instead of the formal `author` field (`ཏཱ་ར་ནཱ་ཐ`); the
    bibliography bullet already had it right. The `<ref>` now matches the

  - In-prose mentions were already correct in v1 for all five commentators (each

    unchanged here.
