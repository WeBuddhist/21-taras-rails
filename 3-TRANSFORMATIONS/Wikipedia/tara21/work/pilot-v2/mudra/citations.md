# Citations — mudra (ཕྱག་རྒྱ), Mode B register revision

This is a **Mode B** (`wiki-article-from-claims-v2`) revision-in-place of the v1 article
at `source_article` above. It never re-opened `2-RAILS/Claims/mudra.md` or any raw
tree-guided claims file: every claim ID, quotation, and `Cite:` target below is carried
forward unchanged from `source_citations` above, which remains the authoritative
resolution record. This file only re-registers what changed under Mode B — the register
of the prose, the quotation count, and the citation cap — not the underlying claims.

Scope is unchanged from the source article (see its own scope note): ཕྱག་རྒྱ as the
ritual hand-gesture attested in this corpus, principally the homage-9 gesture-pair, its
doctrinal reasoning, its divergent readings, its role paired with mantra, and its reuse
as a recurring iconographic template. ཕྱག་རྒྱ་ཆེན་པོ remains out of scope.

## What changed under Mode B

- **Quotations:** 16 verbatim commentary quotations → **2** (plus the root-verse
  quotation in the lead, which is exempt from the budget and unchanged). The 14 cut
  quotations were converted to paraphrase carrying the same claim content, with
  attribution kept only where the source material is a genuine divergence (the
  `གཞུང་ལུགས་སོ་སོའི་བཤད་པ།` section) or a named physical variant (སུང་རབ་སྤྲུལ་སྐུ's
  differing finger configuration in `ངེས་ཚིག`).
- **Register:** the `ངེས་ཚིག`, `དབྱེ་བ།`, `དཀོན་མཆོག་གསུམ་དུ་མཚོན་པའི་རྒྱུ་མཚན།`, and
  `ནུས་པ་དང་ཕྲིན་ལས།` sections — previously sequences of "commentator X says '…'.
  Commentator Y likewise says '…'" — are now connected wikivoice prose stating the
  consensus fact once, with 2–3 representative refs attached. Inline commentator naming
  is now reserved for `གཞུང་ལུགས་སོ་སོའི་བཤད་པ།` (Tāranātha's distinctive readings) and
  for the one genuine physical divergence in `ངེས་ཚིག` (སུང་རབ་སྤྲུལ་སྐུ's variant
  finger-configuration, marked "ཅུང་ཟད་མི་མཚུངས" in both the source and this revision).
- **No new claim IDs.** Every claim ID cited below already appears in `source_citations`.
  Nothing was re-derived from `2-RAILS/Claims/raw/tree-guided/`.
