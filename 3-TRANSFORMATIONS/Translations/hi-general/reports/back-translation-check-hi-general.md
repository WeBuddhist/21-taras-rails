## For the reviewer

- **तारा** means both "Tārā" and "star" in Hindi. In 1-2 तारों is "stars" — natural and correct, but a reader
  may enjoy (or stumble on) the double sense.
- **ग्रह** for གདོན (1-21, 2-5) is flagged in the word list: well known in Hindi in this sense, unlike English
  "graha".
