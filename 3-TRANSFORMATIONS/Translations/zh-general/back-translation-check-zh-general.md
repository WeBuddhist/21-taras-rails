# Back-translation check — Chinese, general grade

Each verse of Chinese draft 2 was translated back into plain, literal English and compared with the fact-checked
English (draft 3) and the commentary consensus. Where they part, the Chinese was fixed against the Tibetan →
Chinese draft 3. The back-translation is literal on purpose, so any drift shows.

**Result:** 17 match · 7 minor (wording could mislead) · 8 differ in meaning. All 15 were fixed.

- **MATCH**: the back-translation says what the English says.
- **MINOR**: the meaning survives, but a reader could take it the wrong way (who does what, or a word the Tibetan does not have).
- **DIFFERS**: the meaning changed, or a commentary-consensus reading from the English fact-check was missing.
