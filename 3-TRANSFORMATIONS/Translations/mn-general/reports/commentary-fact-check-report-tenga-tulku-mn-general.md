#### Chapter I — I-3

He glosses *oṃ* (the nature of the five kāyas and five wisdoms), *rje btsun ma*
(protector and refuge of the three worlds, holding the three vows), *'phags ma*
(raised far above the level of ordinary beings) and *sgrol ma* (she who frees all
beings to the level of buddhahood). "Ум, богд хутагт Дарь эхэд мөргөмүй." ("Om, I
pay homage to the venerable noble Tārā.") *богд* (venerable, holy lord) fits *rje
btsun ma*; *хутагт* (ārya, noble, raised above ordinary beings) fits *'phags ma*;
*Дарь эх* (Tārā, "the Mother who saves") fits *sgrol ma*. The locked pair богд
хутагт is supported. Clean.

Readings that **support** the current Mongolian (including places the back-translation check fixed):
- 1-1: Avalokiteśvara, "the protector of the three worlds", shed many tears; they became a stream, an utpala grew from it, and she was born from its filaments. "Гурван ертөнцийн итгэлтийн нүүрнээс ургасан лянхуагийн / Дэлгэрсэн дохиураас урган гарсан" ("arisen from the opened stamens of the lotus that grew from the face of the Protector of the Three Worlds") has the lotus coming from him, not his face as a lotus (English consensus fix 1). *myur ma* and *dpa' mo* are "acts quickly for beings" and "courage undaunted": "түргэн баатар" ("swift heroine") fits. "Мэлмий нь агшин зуурын цахилгаан мэт" ("whose eyes are like a moment's lightning") fits *skad cig glog* — she sees who declines and who flourishes without obstruction.
- 1-2: The autumn moon is clearest (no cloud, no dust), and the fifteenth-day moon is full. "намрын сар бүрнээ / Дүүрсэн зууг давхарласан мэт нүүрт" ("whose face is like a hundred full autumn moons piled up") fits, with the number right and the locked нүүр for *zhal*.
- 1-3: *dka' thub* = "the perfection of ethical discipline": "сахил" (discipline) is right (draft 3's хатуужил, "hardship", was fixed). *spyod yul* = the perfection of wisdom: "билгийн орон" ("the field of wisdom") names it. "мутраа" ("her hand", singular) fits one lotus in the left hand.
- 1-4: The tathāgatas revere her because she is the Mother, the perfection of wisdom, from which all buddhas are born. "Түүнчлэн ирсний оройн чимэг" ("the crown ornament of the Tathāgata[s]") keeps her as the one they honour.
- 1-5: *'dod* = desire realm, *phyogs* = the seventeen abodes of the form realm, *nam mkha'* = the formless realm. "Хүслийн, өнгөт, өнгөгүй гурван оронг дүүргэгч" ("who fills the three realms of desire, form and formless") fits exactly. The seven worlds are the five abodes of the desire realm plus form and formless: "Долоон ертөнцийг өлмийгөөр гишгэн" ("trampling the seven worlds with her feet") has the number right. "Хоцролгүйгээр хураан татаж чадагч" ("able to draw in all, leaving none behind") fits drawing every being to the higher realms and liberation, none left in the lower realms.
