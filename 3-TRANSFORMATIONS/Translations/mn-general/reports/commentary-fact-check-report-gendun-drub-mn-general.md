Readings that **support** the current Mongolian:
- 1-1: *myur ma* = swift to benefit beings, *dpa' mo* = heroic in turning back the battle of saṃsāra → "түргэн баатар" (swift heroine). "Мэлмий нь агшин зуурын цахилгаан мэт" (whose eyes are like instant lightning) matches "her two eyes, like lightning that flashes in an instant, look on the three worlds". "Дэлгэрсэн дохиураас" (from the opened stamens) matches the sense; see style note.
- 1-2: *kun du gang ba* = the fully full autumn moon, not yet waning → "намрын сар бүрнээ / Дүүрсэн зуу" (a hundred autumn moons completely full). "зуу" (a hundred), not "hundreds", is right.
- 1-3: *dka' thub* = "that is, ethical discipline" → "сахил" (discipline) is right (draft 4 fix). *spyod yul* = "the perfection of wisdom" → "билгийн" (of wisdom) is named. The utpala adorning her hand → "Лянхуагаар мутраа сайтар чимсэн" (who adorned her hand with a lotus).
- 1-4: *rgyal ba'i sras* = "the Victors' children, the bodhisattvas" → "Ялгуусны хөвгүүд" (the Victor's sons), literal and fine. "машид шүтсэн" (deeply served) fits his reverent service "with the crown of the head".
- 1-5: *'dod / phyogs / nam mkha'* = desire, form, formless realms → "Хүслийн, өнгөт, өнгөгүй гурван оронг дүүргэгч" (who fills the three realms of desire, form and formlessness) is exactly his reading (English consensus fix). "Долоон ертөнц" (seven worlds) matches his count: three lower realms, desire-realm gods and humans, form and formless. Pressing with her feet → "өлмийгөөр гишгэн"; *'gugs* without exception → "Хоцролгүйгээр хураан татаж чадагч" (able to draw in without leaving any behind).
- 1-6: the order Indra, Agni, Brahmā, Vāyu is kept → "Хурмаст, Гал тэнгэр, Эсрүн, Салхин тэнгэр". *sna tshogs dbang phyug* is plural → "төрөл бүрийн эрхтэн" (lords of every kind). *'byung po* = "чөтгөр", *ro langs* = "босоолой", *dri za* = "үнэр идэгчид" (gandharvas), *gnod sbyin* hosts = "Хорлогч чуулган" (hosts of yakṣas), all as locked. *mdun nas bstod* → "өмнөөс магтаал өргөгдсөн" (praised from in front).
- 1-7: right leg drawn in (emptiness), left extended (compassion) → "Баруун хумьж зүүнээ сунгасан өлмийгөөр гишгэн" (trampling with right drawn in, left extended). "Оволзсон галын дунд машид бадрагч" (blazing intensely amid swirling fire) is his reading ("haughty amid blazing fire") and keeps *'khrugs*.
- 1-8: *tu re* = "Tārā" → "дүрэ" (Ture, her name). *'jigs pa chen mo* = "the wrathful one" → "их аюулт" (greatly terrifying), not "afraid". *bdud kyi dpa' bo* = the afflictions, and with them the four māras → "Шулмын баатар" (Māra's champion) stands. - 1-14: the palm of the hand striking the ground and the feet stamping → "газрын мандалд мутрын / Алгаар цохиод өлмийгөөр дэвслэгч". The wrathful frown → "Хилэгнэн хөмсөг зангидаж". *rim pa bdun* = "the seven levels of the world" → "Долоон давхарга", the translator decision, is supported.

The ten-syllable mantra has OṂ first and SVĀHĀ last; "Суха болон ум-тай сайтар төгссөнөөр" (perfectly endowed with svāhā and oṃ) keeps the root's order. *sdig pa chen po* → "Их хилэнц" (great sin).
- 1-16: *yi ge bcu pa'i ngag* = oṃ tāre tuttāre ture svāhā → "Арван үсэгт тарни" (the ten-syllable mantra). *dgra* = the enemies of liberation → "Дайсны бие" (the enemy's body). *sgron ma* → "зул" (lamp) follows our root (see Textual notes).

Style notes (not errors; the grammar points are for the native reader):
- 1-1 "дохиур" for *ge sar* (stamens, anthers): the native reader should confirm a general reader takes it as "stamen"; "дэлбээ" is "petal", not the same.
- 1-3 "уснаа ургасан" (grown in its own water): the reflexive *-аа* is odd here; "уснаас ургасан" (grown from water) or the word list's hint "усанд төрсөн лянхуа" (lotus born in water) is clearer. The back-translation report flags "уснаа ургасан" as the older liturgical form; the reviewer decides.
- 1-3 *zhi ba*: Gendun Drub reads it as the pacifying of the perfections' opposites (miserliness, laziness …), not as a seventh item. The Mongolian keeps "амарлингүй" (peace) inside the list, in the root's order. Harmless.
- 1-7 "Баруун хумьж зүүнээ сунгасан" and 1-13 "Баруун сунгаж зүүнээ хумиад": only the left leg takes the reflexive *-ээ*, and the two verses use different verb chains. "Баруунаа хумьж, зүүнээ сунгасан" (1-7) and "Баруунаа сунгаж, зүүнээ хумьсан" (1-13) would be parallel.
- 1-10 "гэрлийн эрхийг": *эрхи* (garland, rosary) and *эрх* (power) have the same object form *эрхийг*, and *эрх* is the locked word for *dbang* two lines later ("эрхэндээ оруулагч"). A reader may hear "spreads the power of light". "гэрлийн эрхис" or "гэрлийн хэлхээ" (a string of light) avoids the clash.
- 1-12 "сарны хавиргаар": the everyday word for the crescent is "хавирган сар"; "хавирган сараар тэргүүнээ чимсэн" (whose head is adorned with the crescent moon) reads more naturally.
- 1-13 and 1-16 *kun nas bskor (rab) dga'*: he reads "disciples with faith who delight in turning the Dharma wheel", and the enemies are theirs (afflictions; self-grasping). "төгс эргэн баясаж" (fully turning and rejoicing) and "төгс эргэн дээд баяссан" can be read as Tārā herself circling; the settled literal reading is "surrounded by joy". "Бүх талаар баясгалангаар хүрээлэгдсэн" (surrounded on all sides by joy) would carry it; the reviewer decides.
- 1-17 "Хум-ын дүрт үр бүхий" (possessing a seed in the form of HŪṂ): the root has *sa bon nyid ma* ("who is the seed itself"). "Хум-ын дүрт үр болсон" (who is the seed in the form of HŪṂ) is a shade closer. Also "Хум-ын": after a final *м* the genitive is usually "-ын/-ийн" joined to the syllable; the reviewer should confirm the hyphen style for mantra syllables (the draft also writes "хум-ээс", "дарэ-г", "хара-г").
- 1-18 **encoding:** "арилгaгч" contains a Latin *a* (U+0061) in place of Cyrillic *а*. It will break search and may render oddly. Replace with Cyrillic "арилгагч".
- 1-19 "Тэмцэл" (struggle, fight) for *rtsod pa* (dispute, quarrel — he says "the disputes of opponents"); "Маргаан" (dispute) is the closer everyday word. "Төгс хуягласан баясгалант сүрээр" (by the joyful splendour fully armoured) is the literal reading; he reads the armour as the peaceful and wrathful mantras held with a joyful one-pointed mind.
- 1-20 "дэлгэрсэн нар сар" (spread-out sun and moon) for *rgyas pa* (full): "дүүрэн нар сар" (full sun and moon) is closer; harmless.
- 2-1 "Охин тэнгэр" (goddess, locked for *lha mo*): Gendun Drub says the goddess is "the goddess Tārā" (*lha mo sgrol ma*). The word list notes that Охин тэнгэр is also the name of Palden Lhamo in Mongolia; his gloss supports the reviewer writing "Дарь эх" here if the clash is real.
- 2-1 "сайтар өгүүлснээр" (by reciting well) for *rab tu brjod*, which he glosses "recites with great faith" (*rab tu dad pas*); "сүсгээр өгүүлснээр" (by reciting with faith) is closer.
- 2-3 "Далан сая ялгуусан бүгдээс" (from all the seventy million Victors): "бүгд" (all) is not in the root; "Далан сая ялгуусаас" is enough. The number (seventy million = *bye ba phrag bdun*) is right.
- 2-4 "Бат орших ба эсвэл хөдлөх": "ба эсвэл" (and or) stacks two conjunctions. "Бат оршигч ч бай, хөдлөгч ч бай" (whether stationary or moving) is cleaner.
- 2-5 grammar: "хороор шаналсан, / Зовлонгийн чуулганыг" makes the masses of suffering the ones "afflicted", not the people (the Nepali needed the same fix). See the ERROR row for a combined fix.
- 2-6 "Тотгор бүгд үгүй болж тус тусдаа дарагдах болтугай": "бүгд" (all) is not in the root (*bgegs rnams*, "the obstacles"); harmless.
