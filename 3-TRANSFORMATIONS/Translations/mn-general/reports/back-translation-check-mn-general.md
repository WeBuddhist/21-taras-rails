## For the reviewer

- **киннара** (1-19) replaces the untranslated миамжи; a Mongolian reader may prefer another established word.
- The traditional forms the model preferred (тэргүүн for the face, уснаа ургасан for the lotus) are the older liturgical Mongolian; the word list chose нүүр and лянхуа for a general reader — flagged picks.
