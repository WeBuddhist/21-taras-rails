Readings that **support** the current Mongolian:
- 1-1: *sgrol ma* = liberates from saṃsāra's suffering, *myur ma* = swift for beings, *dpa' mo* = heroic in conquering the afflictions → "түргэн баатар Дарь эх" ("swift, heroic Tārā") fits. *Spyan … glog* → "Мэлмий нь агшин зуурын цахилгаан мэт" ("whose eyes are like a moment's lightning") fits. *chu skyes zhal gyi*: "from his [Avalokiteśvara's] tears arose a lotus or utpala; she was born from the opening lotus". "Гурван ертөнцийн итгэлтийн нүүрнээс ургасан лянхуагийн / Дэлгэрсэн дохиураас урган гарсан" ("born from the opened stamens of the lotus that grew from the face of the Protector of the Three Worlds") has a lotus arising from him, not a face that is a lotus (the 1-1 error fixed in Phase 2 does not recur).
- 1-2: "a full moon heaped up many hundreds of times" → "бүрнээ / Дүүрсэн зууг давхарласан мэт нүүрт" ("whose face is like a hundred full moons stacked") fits. The stars remain light-givers ("гэрэл бадрагч", "blazing with light").
- 1-3: *phyag … brgyan* = the lotus held in her left hand; "мутраа" ("her hand") is singular and fits. *dka' thub* = *tshul khrims* → "сахил" ("ethical discipline") is exact. *spyod yul nyid ma* = wisdom → "билгийн" ("of wisdom") is named. The list keeps the root order: өглөг, хичээнгүй, сахил, амарлингүй, тэвчээр, дияан, билиг.
- 1-4: *rgyal ba'i sras* = all the bodhisattvas → "Ялгуусны хөвгүүд" ("the Jina's sons") is the literal classical term. *shin tu bsten* = "they carry her on their crown" → "машид шүтсэн" ("deeply relied on / venerated") fits.
- 1-5: *'dod / phyogs / nam mkha'* = the desire, form and formless realms → "Хүслийн, өнгөт, өнгөгүй гурван оронг дүүргэгч" ("who fills the three realms of desire, form and formlessness") is exact. *'jig rten bdun* → "Долоон ертөнц" ("seven worlds"). *'gugs par nus ma* = able to bring kings and others under her power → "хураан татаж чадагч" ("able to draw in / summon") fits. *Tuttāre hūṃ* is the mantra → "дүдарэ хум үсгээр".
- 1-6: Indra ("Хурмаст"), Agni ("Гал тэнгэр"), Brahmā ("Эсрүн") and Vāyu ("Салхин тэнгэр") match, in the root order. *'byung po* → "чөтгөр", *ro langs* → "босоолой", *dri za* (Pañcaśikha etc.) → "үнэр идэгчид" (the exact calque, "smell-eaters"), *gnod sbyin* (Vaiśravaṇa etc.) → "хорлогч". *tshogs* = the eight classes → "чуулган". *mdun nas bstod* → "өмнөөс магтаал өргөгдсөн" (see style note).
- 1-7: posture "right drawn in, left extended" matches ("Баруун хумьж зүүнээ сунгасан"). He says she stands "in the midst of blazing fire"; "Оволзсон галын дунд машид бадрагч" ("who blazes intensely amid the swirling fire") fits, follows the settled "amid fire", and keeps *'khrugs pa* ("оволзсон", "swirling").
- 1-8: *chu skyes zhal* = her own face, like an opened lotus → "Лянхуан нүүрээ" ("her lotus face"); *khro gnyer ldan mdzad* = dwelling as the wrathful one → "хилэгнэн зангидсан" ("wrathfully frowning"). *dgra bo thams cad* = all the adverse side → "Дайсан бүгдийг үлдэлгүй устгагч" ("who slays all enemies without exception").
- 1-9: the left thumb and ring finger hold a lotus at the heart; the three extended fingers symbolise the Three Jewels → "чухаг дээд гурвыг бэлгэдсэн мутарлагийн / Хуруугаар зүрхэн тус газраа сайтар чимсэн" fits. Style notes (not errors; the Mongolian grammar points are for the native reader):
- 1-18 "арилгaгч" contains a **Latin "a"** (U+0061) in place of Cyrillic "а". Search and spell checks will miss the word; replace it with "арилгагч".
- 1-3, 1-15 "амарлингүй": the ending breaks vowel harmony (back-vowel stem амар- with front ү). The word list's hint is "амарлингуй". 1-15 "амарлисан" should likewise be checked (амирласан / амарласан).
- 1-1 "ургасан лянхуагийн … урган гарсан" repeats the verb ург- ("grow") for two different things; "төрсөн" ("born") in line 4 would be cleaner.
- 1-6 "өмнөөс" can be heard as "on behalf of" (хэн нэгний өмнөөс). "өмнө чинь магтаал өргөсөн" ("offered praise before you") is unambiguous. "өргөгдсөн" (passive) with "чуулганаар" is fine.
- 1-9 "чухаг дээд гурав" is classical; a general reader knows "Гурван эрдэнэ" ("the Three Jewels"). "мутарлага" for mudrā should be checked (мудар / мутрын бэлгэ).
- 1-11 "чуулган / Бүгдийг": the head noun needs the accusative too ("чуулганыг бүгдийг", or "чуулган бүгдийг" as one phrase).
- 1-12 "сарны хавиргаар" ("with the rib of the moon"): "хавирган сараар" ("with the crescent moon") is the ordinary phrase. "Авид" for Amitābha should be checked against the usual spelling (Абида / Амитаба).
- 1-14, 1-16 "бутниргэгч": "бут ниргэгч" (two words) is the usual spelling of "smash to pieces".
- 1-19 "шүтүүлсэн" (causative) with an instrumental agent: the passive "шүтэгдсэн" is regular with "-аар" (covered by the 1-19 fix).
- 1-21 "дүрэ дээд хутагт" ("Ture, supreme noble one"): *rab mchog* is "most supreme"; "хутагт" is the lock for *'phags ma* and is not in this line. "дүрэ, эрхэм дээд" ("Ture, most excellent") would be closer. He attributes the destroying to "the power of the ten-syllable mantra"; "Дарагч, дүрэ" fits if Ture is her name (see 1-8).
- 1-22 ends with "билээ." In the root, 1-22 runs on into 2-1 as the object of reciting, and the Mongolian 2-1 has no "үүнийг" ("this") to link back. Drop the full stop, or write "үүнийг сайтар өгүүлснээр" in 2-1.
- 1-0 heading "Үндсэн магтаал" ("the main praise"): his heading is *bstod pa dngos* ("the praise proper"). Fits.
