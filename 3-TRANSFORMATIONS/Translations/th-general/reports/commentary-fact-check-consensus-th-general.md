# Consensus — Thai, four commentaries

Thai went to the commentary check straight after the locked-word swap (no back-translation meaning check first),
so it carries more issues than the other languages did at this stage.

| Commentary | Clean | Verses with errors |
|---|---|---|
| DG | 9/30 | 5 (1-1, 1-3, 1-4, 1-10, 1-18) |
| GD | 11/29 | 12 (1-1, 1-3, 1-4, 1-5, 1-6, 1-8, 1-9, 1-10, 1-13, 1-17, 1-18, 1-20) |
| TN | 8/28 | 9 (1-1, 1-3, 1-4, 1-6, 1-7, 1-10, 1-17, 1-18, 1-20) |
| TT | 10/29 | 7 (1-1, 1-3, 1-4, 1-13, 1-17, 1-18, 1-20) |

**Errors (all fixed):**

- 1-1 (all four): the lotus grows from the Lord's face. The draft gave the Lord a lotus face, the error every machine draft had.
- 1-3 (all four): ตบะ (austerity) was written for discipline (ศีล), and wisdom (ปัญญา) was missing.
- 1-4 (all four): it is the bodhisattvas who attained the perfections, not Tārā.
- 1-18 (all four): the moon is like the gods' lake, not Tārā.
- 1-20: her eyes are like the sun and moon, not Tārā herself.
- 1-13: she dwells amid the fire garland; the draft said she is the fire.
- 1-17: Ture is her name, and the mountains had no verb.
- 1-10: she gives joy and laughs.
- 1-6: many Īśvaras, not one Śiva.
- 1-9 and 1-7: see the next point.

**A recurring pattern (fixed everywhere):**

- "ขอนอบน้อมด้วย X" reads as "I pay homage *by means of* X", which makes the syllables or mudrā the reciter's.
- It is now "ขอนอบน้อมพระผู้ทรง… (ด้วย X)" in 1-2, 1-5, 1-7 and 1-9.
- 1-8 and 1-17 now read "ขอนอบน้อมพระตุเร", following the English decision that Ture is her name.

**Other fixes** where two or more agree, or where the root's wording needs it:

- 1-5: the three realms and สรรพสัตว์.
- 1-11: ความยากไร้ (Tenkal's "destitution").
- 1-14: seven levels.
- 1-16: crushing the enemies' bodies.
- 2-2, 2-3, 2-4: root wording.
- 2-6: the optative, from the English decision.

See the fixes log (21 blocks).
