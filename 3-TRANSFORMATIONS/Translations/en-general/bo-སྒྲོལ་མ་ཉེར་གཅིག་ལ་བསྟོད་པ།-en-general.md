# Praise to the Twenty-One Taras ^0

## Meaning of the Title and the Translator's Homage ^I-0

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^I-1]]

In the language of India: Namaḥ Tārā Ekaviṃśati Stotra Guṇahita Sāka. ^I-1

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^I-2]]

In the language of Tibet: The Homage to Tara with Twenty-One Verses and its Benefits. ^I-2

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^I-3]]

Oṃ! Homage to the Noble and Venerable Tara. ^I-3

## The Actual Praise ^1-0

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-1]]

Homage to Tara, the swift and heroic,
Whose eyes are like a flash of lightning,
Born from the opening stamens of the lotus
That arose from the face of the Lord of the Three Worlds. ^1-1

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-2]]

Homage to her whose face is like
A hundred full autumn moons gathered together,
Blazing with the brilliant light
Of a thousand gathered stars. ^1-2

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-3]]

Homage to her whose hand is adorned
With a gold and blue water-born lotus,
She whose sphere of activity is generosity, diligence, discipline, peace,
Patience, meditative concentration, and wisdom. ^1-3

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-4]]

Homage to the Uṣṇīṣa of the Tathāgatas,
She who acts with infinite, complete victory,
Deeply relied upon by the children of the Victors,
Who have attained every single perfection without exception. ^1-4

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-5]]

Homage to her who, with the syllables tuttare and hum,
Fills the desire, form, and formless realms,
Trampling the seven worlds under her feet,
Able to summon all without exception. ^1-5

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-6]]

Homage to her whom Indra, Agni, Brahmā,
Vāyu, and the various Īśvaras worship,
Praised from before by hosts of spirits,
Vetālas, gandharvas, and the hosts of yaksas. ^1-6

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-7]]

Homage to you who, with the syllables trat and phat,
Utterly destroy the magical wheels of adversaries,
Trampling with right leg drawn in and left extended,
Blazing amidst a swirling mass of fire. ^1-7

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-8]]

Homage to ture, the Great Terrifying One,
Who completely destroys the champions of Mara,
Who forms a frowning expression upon your lotus face,
And slays all enemies without exception. ^1-8

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-9]]

Homage to her whose fingers, in the mudra symbolizing the Three Jewels,
Beautifully adorn her heart,
Her palm adorned with the wheel of all directions without exception,
Radiating a turbulent mass of her own light. ^1-9

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-10]]

Homage to you, who bring supreme joy, whose majestic
Crown ornament spreads garlands of light,
Who, with the great laughter of tuttare,
Brings maras and the world under your power. ^1-10

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-11]]

Homage to you, who have the ability to summon
All the hosts of guardians of the earth,
Who, with a vibrating frown and the syllable hum,
Completely liberate from all destitution. ^1-11

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-12]]

Homage to you, whose head is adorned with a crescent moon,
Whose every ornament blazes intensely,
From whose matted locks Amitābha
Constantly radiates an intense light. ^1-12

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-13]]

Homage to you, who dwell within a garland of fire
Like the blaze at the end of an eon;
With right leg extended and left bent, surrounded by joy,
You completely destroy the armies of enemies. ^1-13

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-14]]

Homage to you, who strike the surface of the earth
With the palms of your hands and trample it with your feet;
With a wrathful frown and the syllable hum,
You shatter the seven levels. ^1-14

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-15]]

Homage to you, who are blissful, virtuous, and peaceful,
Whose very sphere of experience is the peace of nirvāṇa;
Perfectly endowed with the syllables OṂ and SVĀHĀ,
You completely destroy great negativity. ^1-15

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-16]]

Homage to you, surrounded by joy,
Who utterly shatters the bodies of enemies;
With the ten-syllable mantra arranged,
You are the lamp arising from the knowledge-mantra hum. ^1-16

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-17]]

Homage to you, Ture, who stamp your feet,
Whose essence is the seed-syllable in the form of hum;
You cause Mount Meru, Mandara, and Vindhya,
And all the three worlds to tremble. ^1-17

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-18]]

Homage to you, who hold in your hand the moon,
Which has the form of a celestial lake;
By reciting TĀRA twice and the syllable phat,
You eliminate every poison without exception. ^1-18

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-19]]

Homage to you, served by the kings of the hosts of gods,
Relied upon by gods and kinnaras;
With the majesty of your armor of universal joy,
You dispel conflicts and evil dreams. ^1-19

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-20]]

Homage to you, whose two eyes are as brilliant
As the light of the full sun and moon;
By reciting HARA twice and tuttare,
You dispel the most violent infectious diseases. ^1-20

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-21]]

Homage to you, perfectly endowed with the might of peace,
With the three suchnesses set upon you;
You are the supreme Ture who destroys
The hosts of demons, vetālas, and yaksas. ^1-21

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^1-22]]

With this praise of the root mantra,
And these twenty-one homages. ^1-22

## The Benefits of the Praise ^2-0

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^2-1]]

Any intelligent person endowed with perfect devotion to the goddess,
Who recites this with supreme intent, ^2-1

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^2-2]]

Having arisen at dusk and at dawn,
Will be granted complete fearlessness through recollection;
All misdeeds will be thoroughly pacified,
And all lower realms will be utterly destroyed. ^2-2

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^2-3]]

Seventy million Victorious Ones
Will swiftly confer empowerment,
And one will attain even greater excellence than this,
Proceeding to that ultimate state of Buddhahood. ^2-3

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^2-4]]

Their poisons, which are extremely fierce,
Whether stationary or moving,
Even those that have been eaten or drunk,
Are completely eliminated through recollection. ^2-4

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^2-5]]

For those tormented by demons, infectious diseases, and poisons,
The masses of suffering are utterly cast off;
This applies to other sentient beings as well. ^2-5

![[bo-སྒྲོལ་མ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ལ་བསྟོད་པ།#^2-6]]

If recited two, three, or seven times,
Those who desire a child will obtain a child,
Those who desire wealth will obtain wealth,
All desires will be fulfilled,
And may obstacles be absent, each one destroyed. ^2-6

## Colophon ^a-0

The praise to the Blessed Tārā, spoken by the Perfectly Complete Buddha, is completed. ^a-1
