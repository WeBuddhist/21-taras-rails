### Other findings

- **Textual variants, not errors:** at 1-16, DG, GD and TT read *sgrol ma* ("liberator")
  where our root has *sgron ma* ("lamp"); TN reads *sgron ma*. At 1-18/1-20, TN's
  edition has *ha ra* and *ta ra* swapped relative to our root. Our English follows
  our root.
- **Termbase:** 1-12 uses `supreme` for ཤིན་ཏུ (locked to རབ); 1-21 uses `power` for
  མཐུ (the termbase note says "might"); and `lotus_face` needs narrowing to 1-8.
- **Soft notes shared by several:** 1-13/1-16 *kun nas bskor* — GD, TN and TT read
  the "surrounding joy" as a joyful retinue of disciples; 2-1 *rab tu brjod* — GD
  "with great faith", TN "with diligence" (English "with supreme intent").
- **Tool limitation:** `extract_translation.py` captures only the last line of each
  multi-line verse in the transclusion layout. The audits used the grade file's
  `en_text` instead.
