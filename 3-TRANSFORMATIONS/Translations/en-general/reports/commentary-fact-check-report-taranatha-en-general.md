Readings that **support** the current English:
- 1-3 *gser sngo*: "a lotus with a golden stalk and a blue flower". The colours are the lotus's, as the English has them. *spyod yul*: "the perfections are your domain; you are the one who practises them". The English framing fits.
- 1-6 *sna tshogs dbang phyug*: "the various ones renowned as creators or lords" (Rudra, Kāmadeva and others). Plural — "the various Īśvaras" fits.
- 1-8 *bdud kyi dpa' bo* = "Māra's commanders" (*dmag dpon*). "champions of Mara" fits.
- 1-9 *phyogs kyi 'khor lo* = "the realms of the ten directions, pervaded by the light of her body". The English fits.
- 1-14: the harmful beings "dwelling in the seven underground levels" are shattered. "Seven underworld levels" fits.
- 1-17 *'bigs byed*: "following most Tibetan copies, the king of mountains *'bigs byed*". The Sanskrit he saw has Kailāsa; "editions vary, so either is acceptable". A mountain, so "Vindhya" fits.

Textual notes (not errors):
- 1-18 / 1-20: his edition has *ha ra* at 1-18 and *ta ra* at 1-20, the reverse of our root. Our English follows our root.
- 1-4: he reads the Uṣṇīṣa as that of the Tathāgata Vairocana (singular). English "of the Tathāgatas".
- 1-13 *kun nas bskor*: "the various dance postures, encircling". English "surrounded by joy".
- 2-1 *rab tu brjod*: "reciting with diligence" (the Sanskrit has *va*). English "with supreme intent".
