The termbase pass mostly changed spellings and names: DharmaMitra's scholarly forms
(TUTTĀRA, HŪṂ, Tārā, yakṣas) became the plain locked forms, and "zombies" became
"vetālas". The one change of meaning was 1-11's "power" → "ability". The fact-check
changed meaning: who serves whom (1-19), what "rig pa" is (1-16), where the lotus comes
from (1-1), the six perfections (1-3). The glossary given to DharmaMitra barely steered
it: its zero-shot draft (`Dharmamitra/en/`) is about 88% the same as draft 1, and
1-6 still said "Zombies" and "yakṣas" despite the glossary.
