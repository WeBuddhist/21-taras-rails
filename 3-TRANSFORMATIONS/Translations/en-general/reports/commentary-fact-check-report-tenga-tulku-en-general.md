Readings that **support** the current English:
- 1-6: Indra, Agni, Brahmā, Vāyu "and the other great direction-protectors" (plural). The English fits.
- 1-8: "*phyag 'tshal tu re* gives the Venerable One's name". "Homage to ture" as an address fits. *bdud kyi dpa' bo* = "the army of the four māras"; "champions of Mara" fits.
- 1-10: "she has supreme joy in giving all benefit and happiness to beings". The joy is hers, as the English has it.
- 1-14: "the palms of both hands press the earth" (plural). "the palms of your hands" fits.
- 1-15: "OṂ at the beginning and SVĀHĀ at the end". The order is fine.
- 1-19 *rtsod pa* = "quarrels arising from unhappy minds". "conflicts" fits.
- 1-22: "the praise by the root mantra … **and** the twenty-one homages", counted out as 1 + 6 + 7 + 1 + 6. Two items; the English fits.

Style notes (not errors):
- 1-13, 1-16 *kun nas bskor*: "disciples with faith who circle her, delighted"; English "surrounded by joy".
- 2-6 last line: the root is optative (*'joms 'gyur cig*, "may they be destroyed"), and Tenga Tulku glosses it that way. The English has "will be … destroyed".
- 2-3: he reads the seventy million Victors as having empowered Tārā herself. The English ("Will swiftly confer empowerment") leaves the recipient open, so this isn't flagged.
