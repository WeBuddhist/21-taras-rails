## For the reviewer

- The primed run writes the refrain as **वन्दना …गर्ने** ("Homage — she who …") throughout, where the zero-shot
  wrote **वन्दना उनलाई, … मातालाई** ("Homage to her, … to the Mother"). Both are good Nepali liturgy; a native
  reader should confirm the shorter form reads well aloud.
