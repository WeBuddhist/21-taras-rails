Readings that **support** the current Nepali:
- 1-1: *sgrol ma* = liberates from saṃsāra's suffering, *myur ma* = swift for beings, *dpa' mo* = heroic in conquering the afflictions → "तारा, द्रुत र शूरवीर" ("Tārā, swift and heroic") fits. *Spyan … glog* → "नयन क्षणभरको बिजुली जस्ता" ("eyes like a moment's lightning") fits. *chu skyes zhal gyi*: "from his [Avalokiteśvara's] tears arose a lotus or utpala; she was born from the opening lotus". "तीन लोकका नाथको मुखबाट प्रकट कमलको / प्रस्फुटित केशरबाट उत्पन्न" ("born from the opening stamens of the lotus that appeared from the face of the Lord of the Three Worlds") has a lotus arising from him, not a face that is a lotus. That fits.
- 1-2: "a full moon heaped up many hundreds of times" → "सय पूर्ण चन्द्रमाहरू खप्टिए जस्तो" ("like a hundred full moons stacked together") fits. The stars "are examples of countless radiating rays", and "हजारौं ताराहरूको समूहद्वारा / … प्रकाश छर्ने" ("spreading light by hosts of thousands of stars") keeps the stars as light.
- 1-3: *phyag … brgyan* = the lotus held in her left hand; "जसको हात" ("whose hand") is singular and fits. *dka' thub* = *tshul khrims* → "शील" ("ethical discipline") is exact. *spyod yul nyid ma* = wisdom → "प्रज्ञा" is named. The list keeps the root order: दान, वीर्य, शील, शान्ति, क्षान्ति, ध्यान, प्रज्ञा.
- 1-4: *mtha' yas rnam par rgyal ba spyod* = acting victorious over all opponents, māras and enemies → "अनन्त र पूर्ण विजयी आचरण गर्ने" ("who acts in boundless, complete victory") fits. *rgyal ba'i sras* = all the bodhisattvas → "बोधिसत्त्वहरू" names them plainly (the Hindi *जिनपुत्र* question does not arise). *ma lus pha rol phyin pa thob pa'i* = the ten perfections, attained, and "अशेष पारमिताहरू प्राप्त गरेका / बोधिसत्त्वहरू" gives them to the bodhisattvas. *shin tu bsten* = "they carry her on their crown"; "अत्यन्त सेवित" ("deeply served") fits.
- 1-5: *'dod / phyogs / nam mkha'* = the desire, form and formless realms, and "काम, रूप र अरूप लोकहरू भर्ने" ("who fills the desire, form and formless worlds") is exact; *भर्नु* is plain "fill" (the Hindi *पूर्ण करना* problem does not arise). *'jig rten bdun* = the five desire-realm destinies + form realm + formless realm → "सातै लोक" ("all seven worlds"). *Tuttāre hūṃ* is the mantra → "तुत्तारे र हूँ अक्षरद्वारा".
- 1-6: Indra ("इन्द्र"), Agni ("अग्निदेव"), Brahmā ("ब्रह्मा") and Vāyu ("वायुदेव") match, in the root order. *'byung po* = Gaṇapati and the like → "भूत". *ro langs* = Maheśvara and the like → "वेताल". *dri za* = Pañcaśikha etc. → "गन्धर्वहरू". *gnod sbyin* = Vaiśravaṇa etc. → "यक्ष". *tshogs* = the eight classes → "समूह". *mdun nas bstod* → "अगाडि स्तुति गरिएकी" ("praised before her").
- 1-7: *pha rol 'khrul 'khor*: "with her wrathful speech she destroys and turns back the evil applications others have made" → "शत्रुको यन्त्रहरू पूर्ण रूपले नष्ट गर्ने" ("who utterly destroys the enemies' yantras") fits. Posture "right drawn in, left extended" matches ("दाहिने खुम्च्याएको र देब्रे तन्काएको"). He says she stands "in the midst of blazing fire"; "प्रज्वलित अग्निमा अत्यन्त प्रज्वलित हुने" ("who blazes intensely amid blazing fire") fits (and follows the settled "amid fire").
- 1-8: *'jigs pa chen mo*: she terrifies the māras. "महाभयङ्करी" ("the greatly terrifying one") is active. *chu skyes zhal* = her own face, like an opened lotus → "कमल-मुख" ("lotus face"). *dgra bo thams cad* = all the adverse side, the afflictions → "सम्पूर्ण शत्रुहरूलाई अशेष वध गर्ने" ("who slays all enemies without exception").
- 1-9: the left thumb and ring finger hold a lotus at the heart; the three extended fingers symbolise the Three Jewels → "त्रिरत्नको प्रतीक मुद्राको / औंलाहरूद्वारा हृदयमा … सजिएकी" fits. One wheel on the palm of the right hand in the supreme-giving gesture → "जसको हत्केला सम्पूर्ण दिशाहरूको चक्रले सजिएको" ("whose palm is adorned with the wheel of all directions") is singular and fits.
- 1-10: *bzhad pas rab bzhad tuttāra* = the mantra proclaims the sound of laughter → "तुत्तारेको अट्टहासद्वारा" ("by the loud laughter of tuttāre"). *bdud dang 'jig rten dbang du mdzad* → "मार र लोकलाई वशमा गर्ने" ("who brings Māra and the world under her power"), with no added "all".
- 1-11: *thams cad* is on the hosts ("समूहहरू / सबैलाई", "all the hosts"), where the root has it.
- 1-12: a first-day moon adorns her head → "अर्धचन्द्र" (conventionally "the crescent"). Amitābha amid her locks "radiates immeasurable rays for beings" → "जटाको बीचमा अमिताभबाट, / निरन्तर अत्यन्त प्रकाश" ("from Amitābha amid her locks, constant intense light") keeps Amitābha as the source of the light.
- 1-13: the fire at the end of the eon, when seven suns rise → "कल्पको अन्त्यको अग्नि". The garland (not she) blazes like it. The posture is the reverse of 1-7 → "दाहिने तन्काएको र देब्रे खुम्च्याई" fits. *dgra yi dpung* = all adverse things → "शत्रुको सेना".
- 1-14: "she strikes the ground with her **left hand**": "हातको / हत्केलाले" ("with the palm of the hand") is singular and fits (the Hindi plural-hands row does not recur). *bsnun* → "हिर्काउँदै" ("striking"), *brdung* → "चरणले प्रहार गर्ने" ("pounding with her feet").
- 1-15: *bde ma* = endowed with uncontaminated bliss, *dge ma* = free of the afflictions, *zhi ma* = suffering pacified, *mya ngan 'das* = conceptions exhausted → "सुखी, कल्याणी, शान्ता, / निर्वाणको शान्ति जसको आचरणको क्षेत्र हो" fits. "स्वाहा र ॐ" follows the root order.
- 1-16: *yi ge bcu pa'i ngag* = the peaceful mantra *oṃ tāre tuttāre ture svāhā* → "दश अक्षरको मन्त्र". *rig pa hūṃ* = the wrathful (vidyā) mantra → "विद्या हूँ" fits. "दीप" ("lamp") follows our root's *sgron ma* (see Textual notes).
- 1-17: from HŪṂ she is generated as the wrathful form → "हूँ को स्वरूपको बीज भएकी" ("who is the seed in the form of HŪṂ"). *'jig rten gsum* = the three realms → "तीनै लोक". She is the one who shakes them.
- 1-18: "she holds in her hand the moon disc, which is **like a celestial lake**; *ri dwags rtags can* is the moon" → "देवतालको स्वरूपको / चन्द्रमा हातमा धारण गर्ने" ("who holds in her hand the moon, in the form of the gods' lake"). The simile is on the moon, as it should be. The TĀRA mantra removes stationary and moving poisons; "सम्पूर्ण विषहरूलाई अशेष रूपले" ("all poisons without exception") covers both, since *विष* is any poison.
- 1-19: "the lords of those hosts of gods, and Druma king of the kinnaras, worship at her feet" → "देवसमूहका राजाहरू, / देवता र किन्नरहरूद्वारा सेवित" ("served by the kings of the god-hosts, by gods and kinnaras"). Plural *राजाहरू*, and the one *द्वारा* covers the whole list, so the kings serve her. *go cha* = the armour of deity-body and mantra → "कवच". *rtsod* = the tīrthikas' disputes → "विवाद"; bad dreams → "नराम्रो सपना".
- 1-20: the two eyes as sun and moon, light arising from them → "जसका दुई नेत्र पूर्ण सूर्य र चन्द्रमा जस्तै / अत्यन्त उज्यालो प्रकाशले चम्किन्छन्" ("whose two eyes shine with bright light like the full sun and moon"). *rims nad* → "महामारी" ("epidemic").
- 1-21: *de nyid gsum*: OṂ ĀḤ HŪṂ set at her body, speech and mind → "तीन तत्त्वहरू स्थापित भएकी" ("she on whom the three tattvas are set"; see style note). *zhi ba'i mthu* = the might of pacifying the afflictions → "शान्तिको शक्ति". *gdon, ro langs, gnod sbyin* → "ग्रह, वेताल र यक्ष" in the root order (ग्रह kept and flagged, Tenkal's decision).

Style notes (not errors; the Nepali grammar points are for the native reader):
- 1-1 "शूरवीर" ("hero") leans masculine for *dpa' mo* ("heroine"). "वीरा" or "वीराङ्गना" is available if a native reader hears it as male.
- 1-3 "जसको आचरणको क्षेत्र हो" ("is the field of her conduct") makes all seven items her sphere, where he reads *spyod yul* as wisdom itself. Wisdom is named, so this is acceptable. "जलज कमल" ("water-born lotus") is redundant but follows the root's *chu nas skyes kyi padma*.
- 1-4 and 1-6: only these two homages end in "…लाई" ("to her who …"): "सेवितलाई", "पूजितलाई". In 1-6 the second half then switches to "स्तुति गरिएकी". A native reader should make the refrain consistent.
- 1-5 and 1-11 *'gugs*: he reads it as bringing under her power ("able to bring kings and others under control"; the protectors "act as her messengers"). "तान्नु" is everyday "pull". "आकर्षित गरी वशमा ल्याउन" ("draw in and bring under control") is available. 1-5 "तान्न सक्ने क्षमता भएकी" ("who has the capacity to be able to pull") says "able" twice; "तान्न समर्थ भएकी", as in 1-11, would be cleaner.
- 1-7 "शत्रुको यन्त्रहरू": with a plural head noun Nepali wants "शत्रुका यन्त्रहरू". *'khrugs pa* ("swirling") is not rendered; he does not gloss it.
- 1-8 "शत्रुहरूलाई अशेष वध गर्ने": "शत्रुहरूको अशेष वध गर्ने" is the more standard construction.
- 1-9 "आफ्नै प्रकाशका उर्लँदा समूह फैलाउने" ("spreading the surging masses of her own light") keeps the light but not his "outshines other lights". Fine.
- 1-10: he explains the crown ornament as "garlands of many jewels"; the Nepali keeps the root's "प्रकाशको माला" ("garland of light"). Fine.
- 1-12 "अर्धचन्द्रले मुकुट सजिएकी" is loose; "जसको मुकुट अर्धचन्द्रले सजिएको छ" ("whose crown is adorned with the crescent") or "अर्धचन्द्रले सजिएको मुकुट भएकी" would be cleaner. He reads *brgyan pa thams cad shin tu 'bar* as the moon's light blazing; "सम्पूर्ण आभूषणहरू" ("all the ornaments") follows the root. Fine.
- 1-13 "दाहिने तन्काएको र देब्रे खुम्च्याई" mixes a participle and a conjunctive form, and the line is very long. "दाहिने तन्काई देब्रे खुम्च्याई," with "चारैतिरबाट आनन्दले घेरिएकी" on its own line, would read better.
- 1-15 "सुखी" is everyday "happy, content". He means "endowed with uncontaminated bliss"; "सुखमयी" is more elevated. *sdig pa chen po* he glosses as "others' afflictions to be abandoned", destroyed "by her nature as dharmakāya"; "महान् पाप" ("great sin") follows the root. Fine.
- 1-17 "हूँ को" should be written "हूँको". The mountain list lacks its object marker and link: "मेरु, मन्दर र विन्ध्य पर्वतलाई, / अनि तीनै लोकलाई हल्लाउने" would be cleaner ("तीनै" already carries the plural, so "लोकहरू" is not needed).
- 1-18 "देवतालको" can be misparsed as "देवता + …". "देव-तालको" or "देवसरोवरको" ("of the gods' lake") is clearer.
- 1-20 "भयंकर" (also 2-4) and 1-8 "भयङ्करी": one spelling should be chosen. 1-1 "नयन" and 1-20 "नेत्र" both render *spyan*; both are fine.
- 1-21 "तीन तत्त्वहरू स्थापित भएकी" is loose and could be heard as "she who is established [as] three tattvas". The English consensus fix (4 of 4) was "set upon her". "जसमा तीन तत्त्व स्थापित छन्," ("on whom the three tattvas are set") is unambiguous. He attributes the destroying to "the power of the ten-syllable mantra"; "नष्ट गर्ने तुरे परम उत्तम भएकी" ("Ture, supremely excellent, who destroys") fits if Ture is her name (see 1-8).
- 1-22 closes with a danda ("…वन्दनाहरू।"). In the root, 1-22 is a noun phrase that runs on into 2-1, and the Nepali 2-1 has no "यसको" ("this") to link back, so the object of "पाठ गर्नाले" ("by reciting") is lost. Drop the danda (end with a comma), or write "यसको राम्ररी पाठ गर्नाले" in 2-1.
- 1-0 heading "मूल स्तुति" ("the main praise"): his heading is *bstod pa dngos* ("the praise proper"). Fits.

Readings that **support** the current Nepali:
- 2-1: devotion to *lha mo* with body, speech and mind → "देवीप्रति … भक्तिले युक्त" ("endowed with devotion toward the goddess"). He does not identify *lha mo*, so "देवी" is not contradicted. *rab tu brjod* (his *rab dang*) → "राम्ररी पाठ" ("recites well") fits either.
- 2-2 *srod dang tho rangs*: at dusk the wrathful body is recollected, at dawn the peaceful body → "साँझ र बिहान" ("at dusk **and** at dawn"). No "always" is added. The object of recollection is her ("उहाँको स्मरण", "remembering her"), as he says: the deity's body. *ngan 'gro* = "the result of the lower realms is pacified" → "दुर्गति".
- 2-3: *bye ba phrag bdun* = seventy million, and "सात करोड" (7 × 10 million) is exact. *rgyal ba* → "बुद्धहरू" ("buddhas"), which avoids the *जिन* question (Tenkal's Hindi decision). The empowerment is conferred on the reciter; "चाँडै अभिषेक दिइनेछ" ("empowerment will soon be given") fits. *'di las che ba* = "not only this life, but more" → "यसभन्दा पनि महानता प्राप्त गरी" ("attaining greatness even beyond this"). *sangs rgyas go 'phang mthar thug* → "अन्तिम बुद्ध पदमा पुग्नेछ" ("will reach the final state of buddhahood"), singular.
- 2-4: stationary and moving poisons (aconite-type plant poisons; snakes and scorpions), eaten or drunk → "स्थावर होस् वा जङ्गम" is the exact Sanskrit pair (*sthāvara / jaṅgama*), and "खाएको र पिएको भए तापनि" ("even if eaten and drunk") has no "accidentally".
